About Totally Cape Cod

A guide to beaches, eats, stays and events from Bourne to Provincetown. We're an intermediary: we don't make anything up. We research, confirm and post, and link the source wherever we can.

Why we built this

Information about Cape Cod's beaches, restaurants and things to do is scattered across town websites, old PDFs and word of mouth. We wanted one place that answers the question every visitor actually asks: "What should I do today?"

What's inside

  • Conditions — water temps from three sensors, NOAA tide times, Google Maps bridge delays, and beach parking reports from other visitors (shown for 3 hours after they're posted).
  • Places — beaches, restaurants, stays and things to do, with rules and details linked to the town, park or business that publishes them.
  • Events — pulled twice a day from venue, chamber and town calendars, each credited and linked to its source.
  • A free app — add it to your home screen and check the Cape from your pocket.

How we stay free

Totally Cape Cod is free to use. We keep the lights on through affiliate links (when you book a hotel or tour). See our Privacy Policy and Terms for the details.
