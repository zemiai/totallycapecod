Detours

Cape Cod Detours

Two beaches and a trail, with the parking, dog and tide rules linked to their sources.

Each stop below lists only what we could confirm with the town or the National Park Service, with a link so you can check it before you go. Rules change from season to season.

Ballston Beach

Truro · Ocean beach at the end of South Pamet Road

From the 3rd Saturday of June to Labor Day, the 65-space lot needs a Truro beach sticker (no day passes), and dogs aren’t allowed 9 AM–6 PM. There are no lifeguards.

Sources: Town of Truro: Ballston Beach · Town of Truro: pet rules

Paine's Creek Beach

Brewster · Cape Cod Bay

Brewster requires a beach parking permit from June 15 through the Sunday of Labor Day. A portable toilet is available from Memorial Day through the October holiday weekend.

Source: Town of Brewster: beach information

Great Island Trail to Jeremy Point

Wellfleet · Cape Cod National Seashore

The National Seashore’s most difficult trail: up to 8.8 miles round trip, mostly soft sand, out to the Jeremy Point overlook. Portions are submerged at high tide, so bring water and plan around the tide.

Source: NPS: Great Island Trail

More ideas for a slower day

1. Watch the sunrise from a harbor. Bring coffee and an extra layer.
2. Visit a bookstore or a gallery when the weather turns.
3. Walk a harbor after dinner.
4. Bike the Cape Cod Rail Trail, a paved trail through six Cape towns (Mass DCR).
5. Drive Route 6A, named a National Scenic Byway in 2021 (Cape Cod Commission).
6. Stay for sunset with nowhere else to be.
