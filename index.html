In the app

What the free app does

One free web app, updated through the day.

  • Check beach parking reports

    Parking reports from other beachgoers, shown for 3 hours after they're posted.

  • Plan a beach walk

    Water temps from three sensors and today's tides from NOAA's Chatham station, for 27 mapped beaches.

  • Check the bridges

    Sagamore & Bourne delays from Google Maps traffic, checked several times a day.

  • Find dinner

    What's open near you, going by Google's listed hours — lobster rolls, clam shacks and more.

  • Keep the kids busy

    Today's events and a 110-stamp summerCape passport.

  • Ask the AI guide

    An AI assistant that answers from the app's current data, like water temps, events and bridge traffic.

  • Check the water

    Water temps from the nearest of three sensors, with the time each was checked.
