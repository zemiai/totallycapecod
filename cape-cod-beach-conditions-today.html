Cape Cod Beach Conditions Today

Water temps by beach, from the nearest sensor · parking reports in the app

🍂 It's the off-season. At the six Cape Cod National Seashore beaches, lifeguards are typically on duty only from late June through Labor Day, and apart from the Salt Pond and Province Lands visitor centers, park restrooms are open seasonally (National Park Service). Town beaches set their own seasons, so check the town and follow posted signs.

Each beach shows the latest water temperature from its nearest working sensor — NOAA's Woods Hole station, NOAA buoy 44020 in Nantucket Sound or buoy 44090 in Cape Cod Bay (Woods Hole Group/NERACOOS) — with the time we checked it. There's no sensor on the ocean side, so Atlantic-facing beaches show the Cape Cod Bay reading and the water there may differ. For parking, the app shows reports from other beachgoers, kept for 3 hours after they're posted.
