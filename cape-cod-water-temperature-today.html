Cape Cod Water Temperature Today

Readings from three sensors, matched to each beach

Each beach below shows the latest reading from its nearest working sensor: NOAA's Woods Hole station (8447930), NOAA buoy 44020 in Nantucket Sound or buoy 44090 in Cape Cod Bay (owned and maintained by Woods Hole Group/NERACOOS; NOAA's National Data Buoy Center publishes its data). There's no sensor on the ocean side, so Atlantic-facing beaches such as Nauset, Coast Guard, Marconi and Race Point show the Cape Cod Bay reading — the water there isn't measured and may be warmer or colder.

🌊 The National Weather Service defines cold water as 70°F or lower and explains the risk of cold shock on its Hazard of Cold Water Activities page.
