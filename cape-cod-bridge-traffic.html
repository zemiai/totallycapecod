Sagamore & Bourne — delays leaving the Cape, from Google Maps traffic

The Sagamore and Bourne bridges are the two highway bridges over the Cape Cod Canal. This page shows Google Maps traffic for the off-Cape direction (leaving the Cape) of each bridge, checked several times a day: the extra minutes traffic adds to the crossing, per Google Maps, and the average speed over the measured stretch. We don't measure the on-Cape direction, so check a live traffic map if you're heading onto the Cape.
