How to use this guide

Each beach lists only the rules and facilities we confirmed with the town or the National Park Service, with a link to that page. Rules change from season to season, so check the linked page before you go. For today's water temperature at each beach, see Water Temps Today.

Outer Cape beaches

Race Point Beach

Town: Provincetown (Cape Cod National Seashore)
Water: the Park Service notes swift currents and deeper water close to shore
Facilities: seasonal restrooms; lifeguards typically on duty late June through Labor Day
Dogs: 6-ft leash where allowed; not on lifeguard-protected beaches in summer

Sources: NPS: Race Point Beach · NPS: guide to beaches · NPS: pets

Coast Guard Beach

Town: Eastham (Cape Cod National Seashore)
Parking: mid-June to Labor Day the beach lot is for Eastham residents and handicap placards; park at Little Creek and take the shuttle.
Facilities: seasonal restrooms, a wheelchair-accessible ramp to the beach, and lifeguards typically on duty late June through Labor Day

Sources: NPS: Coast Guard Beach · NPS: guide to beaches

Ballston Beach

Town: Truro (ocean beach at the end of South Pamet Road)
Parking: 65-space lot; Truro beach sticker required from the 3rd Saturday of June to Labor Day, and no day passes are sold
Facilities: no lifeguards; portable toilet in season
Dogs: not allowed 9 AM–6 PM, 3rd Saturday of June to Labor Day

Sources: Town of Truro: Ballston Beach · Town of Truro: pet rules

Head of the Meadow

Town: Truro (Cape Cod National Seashore)
Facilities: in summer, restrooms or vault toilets

Source: NPS: guide to beaches

Mid Cape beaches

Mayflower Beach

Town: Dennis (Cape Cod Bay)
Parking and rules: check with the town before you go

Craigville Beach

Town: Centerville, in Barnstable (Nantucket Sound)
Parking and rules: check with the town before you go

Sandy Neck Beach

Town: Barnstable (Cape Cod Bay)
Dogs: leashed year-round; not allowed on the public bathing beach (Bodfish Beach) May 15 to September 15
Off-road driving: an ORV permit is required year-round
Facilities: restrooms and changing facilities, and a concession stand

Sources: Town of Barnstable: Sandy Neck FAQ · Town of Barnstable: Sandy Neck public beach

Parking and rules: check with the town before you go

First Encounter Beach

Cape Cod beach parking & dog FAQ

Are dogs allowed on Cape Cod National Seashore beaches?
Where pets are allowed, the National Park Service requires a 6-foot leash. In summer, pets are not permitted on lifeguard-protected beaches, and some areas close to pets from April 1 to October 15 for nesting shorebirds. Town beaches set their own rules.
Source: NPS: pets
Can I buy a daily parking pass for Ballston Beach in Truro?
No. The Town of Truro does not sell a daily beach sticker for Ballston. From the third Saturday of June to Labor Day, its 65-space lot needs a Truro beach sticker.
Source: Town of Truro: Ballston Beach
Where do I park for Coast Guard Beach in Eastham in summer?
From mid-June to Labor Day, the beach lot is restricted to Eastham residents and vehicles with handicap placards. The National Park Service says the primary parking is at Little Creek, where a shuttle runs to the beach.
Source: NPS: Coast Guard Beach
Can I buy a daily parking pass at First Encounter Beach in Eastham?
Yes. In season, the Town of Eastham sells a $30 daily pass per vehicle at First Encounter Beach, cash or check only. An Eastham beach sticker also works.
Source: Town of Eastham: beach parking & daily passes
