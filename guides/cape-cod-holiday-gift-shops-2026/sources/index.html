How we fact-check

How this list was made

Shops come from the gift or shopping categories of town chambers of commerce. The categories are:

  • Brewster: Brewster Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [1]
  • Chatham: Chatham Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [2]
  • Dennis: Dennis Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [3]
  • Falmouth: Falmouth Chamber of Commerce, Falmouth Guidebook 2026 (PDF), page 105, “GIFTS & HOME ACCESSORIES” [4]. The guidebook says its listings are based on the chamber’s database as of April 15, 2026 [4].
  • Hyannis: Greater Hyannis Chamber of Commerce, “Shops: Clothing, Home Furnishing, Gifts, Food” [5]
  • Mashpee: Mashpee Chamber of Commerce member directory, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” filter [6]
  • Orleans: Orleans Chamber of Commerce, “Wedding Gifts” [7]
  • Provincetown: Provincetown Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping” [8]
  • Sandwich: Sandwich Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts, Clothing & Accessories” [9]
  • Truro: Truro Chamber of Commerce, “Shop” [10]
  • Wellfleet: Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [11]
  • Yarmouth: Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [12]

Each shop’s street address comes from its own pages, and each entry cites it. A chamber category is not a recommendation, and it does not promise a gift counter: where a page describes tastings, a museum or a studio visit, the entry says so. The order is alphabetical, by town and then by shop name, ignoring a leading “The”. Each shop was checked on November 15, 2026 for published hours, recent posts or a stated reopening date.

This list has no Harwich shop and one Yarmouth shop because checking of those towns’ chamber listings was not finished by November 15, 2026. No shop in Eastham or Bourne was confirmed from its own pages.

Hours change, and many shops publish none. Where a shop gives hours without a date, we say so. Where a shop’s site still shows a spring or summer label, we do not repeat the hours.
