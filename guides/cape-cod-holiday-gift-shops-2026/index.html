Cape Cod Holiday Gift Shops 2026: A Town-by-Town List, Checked Shop by Shop

Each of the 32 chamber-listed shops was checked on November 15, 2026 for published hours, recent posts or a stated reopening date.

By Versel JohnsonPublished November 15, 2026How we fact-check

Holiday shopping is mostly the art of remembering what was on the list. This guide sets out 32 Cape Cod shops town by town, with each shop’s address, its hours where it publishes them, and the latest sign of activity we found on its own pages. A list is a fine thing to have, particularly when it is not still on the kitchen counter.

How this list was made

Shops come from the gift or shopping categories of town chambers of commerce. The categories are:

  • Brewster: Brewster Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [1]
  • Chatham: Chatham Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [2]
  • Dennis: Dennis Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [3]
  • Falmouth: Falmouth Chamber of Commerce, Falmouth Guidebook 2026 (PDF), page 105, “GIFTS & HOME ACCESSORIES” [4]. The guidebook says its listings are based on the chamber’s database as of April 15, 2026 [4].
  • Hyannis: Greater Hyannis Chamber of Commerce, “Shops: Clothing, Home Furnishing, Gifts, Food” [5]
  • Mashpee: Mashpee Chamber of Commerce member directory, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” filter [6]
  • Orleans: Orleans Chamber of Commerce, “Wedding Gifts” [7]
  • Provincetown: Provincetown Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping” [8]
  • Sandwich: Sandwich Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts, Clothing & Accessories” [9]
  • Truro: Truro Chamber of Commerce, “Shop” [10]
  • Wellfleet: Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [11]
  • Yarmouth: Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [12]

Each shop’s street address comes from its own pages, and each entry cites it. A chamber category is not a recommendation, and it does not promise a gift counter: where a page describes tastings, a museum or a studio visit, the entry says so. The order is alphabetical, by town and then by shop name, ignoring a leading “The”. Each shop was checked on November 15, 2026 for published hours, recent posts or a stated reopening date.

This list has no Harwich shop and one Yarmouth shop because checking of those towns’ chamber listings was not finished by November 15, 2026. No shop in Eastham or Bourne was confirmed from its own pages.

Hours change, and many shops publish none. Where a shop gives hours without a date, we say so. Where a shop’s site still shows a spring or summer label, we do not repeat the hours.

Before you go

Every shop here was checked on one day, November 15, 2026, and hours can change after that.

  • FabVilla says it is closed on Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving and Christmas [58].
  • The Brewster Book Store’s page lists November 26th as closed [13].
  • The Cape Cod Museum of Natural History lists Thanksgiving Day, November 26th, and December 21st to 26th as closed [22]. These are museum closures; we did not confirm the shop’s hours.
  • Nut House Candle Company’s Instagram profile reads “CLOSED! Reopen November 27th” [39].
  • Truro Vineyards’ fall hours run “through just before Christmas” [81], and no hours for after Christmas are listed.
  • Jobi Pottery and Gallery, HandCraft House Gallery, Masterson Made and Oceana show more than one address or town across their own pages. The entries give both.

Quick answers

Which shops on this list say they are open daily or seven days a week?

Five shops use that kind of wording. The Brewster Book Store says it is open 10 AM to 5 PM, seven days a week [14], and its page lists November 26th as closed [13]. The Brewster Store’s site says it is open seven days a week year round [16], while its Instagram profile reads “Open 364 Days a Year!” [17]. Kandy Korner Gifts in Hyannis lists daily hours of 10 AM to 6 PM [51]. HandCraft House Gallery in Brewster lists daily hours of 10 AM to 4 PM [26]. Bass River Mercantile in South Yarmouth lists 10 to 5, Monday through Sunday, with its own caveat: “On somedays we’re not here at all, but lately we’ve been here a lot….unless we’re not here.” [86]

Which shop on this list reopens on a stated date?

Nut House Candle Company in Dennis Port. Its Instagram profile reads “CLOSED! Reopen November 27th” [39].

Which shops say they are closed on a holiday?

FabVilla in Mashpee says it is closed on Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving and Christmas [58]. The Brewster Book Store’s page lists November 26th as closed [13]. The Cape Cod Museum of Natural History lists Thanksgiving Day, November 26th [22], and December 21st to 26th [22] as closed; those are museum closures, not confirmed for its shop.

Does Truro Vineyards publish hours for after Christmas?

Not on the visit page we read. Its fall hours run “through just before Christmas” [81], and the page lists no hours for after Christmas.

Which shops publish hours with a fall or other season label?

Labelled fall: the Brewster Book Store’s “Fall Store Hours” table [13], Truro Vineyards’ “Fall Hours (through just before Christmas)” [81], DRIFT Wellfleet’s “Late Fall Hours” [83], Masterson Made’s “Fall Hours” [47], and the Cape Cod Museum of Natural History’s “Fall 2026 Hours”, which are museum hours [22]. Labelled by month: Stage Stop Candy’s “Showroom Hours: October - December” [44] and Provincetown Fudge Factory’s “NOVEMBER STORE HOURS” [72]. Ross Coppelman’s site still shows a “Spring Hours” label [42]. Adorn Cape Cod’s hours page is marked “updated 9.13.26” [61] but carries no season label.
