Cape Cod Gift Shops Open for the Holidays 2026: A Town-by-Town List

32 Cape Cod gift shops checked on November 15, 2026, listed town by town with published hours, phone numbers and the proof we found that each is open.

By Versel JohnsonPublished November 15, 2026How we fact-check

This is a town-by-town list of Cape Cod gift shops to check before your 2026 holiday shopping. It gives each shop’s address when we could confirm one, the hours the shop itself publishes, a phone number where we found one, and the proof we found that the shop is open. Every list has one person on it who has said they want nothing. Plan accordingly.

How this list was made

Shops come from the gift categories of town chambers of commerce. Where a chamber has no gift category, we used its general shopping list. The categories are:

  • Brewster: Brewster Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [1]
  • Chatham: Chatham Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [2]
  • Dennis: Dennis Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [3]
  • Falmouth: Falmouth Chamber of Commerce, “GIFTS & HOME ACCESSORIES” [4]
  • Hyannis: Hyannis Area Chamber of Commerce, “Shops: Clothing, Home Furnishing, Gifts, Food” [5]
  • Mashpee: Mashpee Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [6]
  • Orleans: Orleans Chamber of Commerce, “Wedding Gifts” [7]
  • Provincetown: Provincetown Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping” [8]
  • Sandwich: Sandwich Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts, Clothing & Accessories” [9]
  • Truro: Truro Chamber of Commerce, “Shop” [10]
  • Wellfleet: Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [11]
  • Yarmouth: Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [12]

Orleans uses a “Wedding Gifts” category. Mashpee, Truro and Yarmouth use general shopping lists, and Provincetown’s category is simply “Shopping.”

We kept only shops with a walk-in storefront on the Cape. Each shop was checked on November 15, 2026, against its own website or its own social media pages. A chamber listing is not a recommendation, and the order here is alphabetical (by town, then by shop name, ignoring a leading “The”), not a ranking.

Coverage is uneven. Harwich has no shop on the list and Yarmouth has one, because those chambers’ directories could not be read. Eastham and Bourne have none confirmed.

Hours change, and many shops publish none. Where a shop gives hours without a date, we say so. Where a shop’s site still shows a spring or summer label, we do not repeat the hours. Confirm with the shop before you drive.

Quick answers

Which Cape Cod gift shops on this list say they are open every day?

Five shops say so on their own sites, none with a date. The Brewster Book Store says “We are open 10 am-5 pm, 7 days a week!” [46]. The Brewster Store says “We are open seven day a week year round.” [47]. Bass River Mercantile in South Yarmouth lists “General hours are 10-5 Monday through Sunday.” [45]. Kandy Korner Gifts in Hyannis lists “Daily Hours - 10am - 6pm” [29]. HandCraft House Gallery in Brewster lists “Open Daily 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM” [51].

Which shop on this list reopens on a stated date?

Nut House Candle Company in Dennis Port. Its Instagram profile says “HOURS: CLOSED! Reopen November 27th” [58].

Which shops say they are closed on a holiday?

FabVilla in Mashpee says “Closed- Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving, Christmas” [64]. The Brewster Book Store’s hours page lists “Upcoming Dates Open Close * November 26th Closed” [13].

Does Truro Vineyards publish December hours?

No. Its site says “We are OPEN YEAR ROUND Fall Hours (through just before Christmas) Monday-Saturday 11-5 Sunday 12-5” and lists no other hours [75].

Which shops publish hours labelled for this fall?

Provincetown Fudge Factory’s Facebook post gives November store hours [38]. Adorn Cape Cod’s hours page is marked “updated 9.13.26” [66]. The Cape Cod Museum of Natural History’s hours page lists “Fall 2026 Hours”, though these are museum hours [50].

Which chamber lists did you use for Orleans and Mashpee?

The Orleans Chamber of Commerce has no gift category, so we used its “Wedding Gifts” category [7]. The Mashpee Chamber of Commerce has none either, so we used its “Shopping & Specialty Retail” list [6].
