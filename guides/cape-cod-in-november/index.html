November's normal mean temperature at Hyannis is 45.4°F 1.

Storms. The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs through November 30 7. The National Weather Service says nor'easters, named for their northeast winds along the coast, can happen any time of year but are most frequent and most violent between September and April 6.

If you're walking a beach or trail, plan to be back at the car by then.

Beaches and the National Seashore in November

Cape Cod National Seashore is open year-round, though from November through April some visitor facilities are closed 10. When we checked on November 10, 2026, the Park Service's current-conditions page showed 8:

  • Beaches: Seashore beaches such as Head of the Meadow and Herring Cove are listed as open, but restrooms and showers are closed (the vault toilets in those two beaches' parking lots are open year-round), and beach entrance fee collection and annual pass sales have ended for the season 8. Seashore entrance fees are charged only seasonally, at park beaches 9.
  • Lifeguards: they're typically on duty at the six Seashore beaches only from late June through Labor Day 10.
  • Visitor centers: Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham, the Seashore's year-round visitor center 11, is listed as open from 9 AM to 4:30 PM 8. The Province Lands Visitor Center in Provincetown is open May through October 11 and has closed for the season, though its outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting 811.
  • Trails: all eleven walking trails in the park are listed as open 8. Leashed pets are allowed on the Nauset and Province Lands bike trails from November 1 through April 30, and on the Head of the Meadow Bike Trail year-round 8.

Hunting season. Hunting for deer, upland game and migratory waterfowl is permitted in the Seashore, generally in accordance with Massachusetts state hunting regulations, and all hunting is prohibited from March 1 through August 31, except for the spring turkey hunt 12. Hunting and loaded weapons are prohibited on or within 500 feet of established bike and official nature trails 12.

If you're walking off-trail in the woods this time of year, wearing bright colors is common sense.

Town beaches. Off-season rules are set town by town. In Eastham, for example, stickers or day passes were required at its bay beaches, freshwater ponds and pull-off areas only from June 20, 2026, through Labor Day, September 7 13.

Check each town's page before you park. For current conditions, see Cape Cod beach conditions today.

Ferries, whale watches and buses: what's still running

Island ferries. The Steamship Authority runs daily, year-round service from Cape Cod to Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket 14; its current Martha's Vineyard schedule runs from October 23, 2026, to January 5, 2027 14. Hy-Line Cruises has run year-round Hyannis–Nantucket service since December 1995 16, but its Hyannis–Martha's Vineyard ferry has ended for the season 15.

Boston and Plymouth to Provincetown. Bay State Cruise Company's fast ferry runs weekends only through November 15, 2026, and it says trips are 25 to 35 minutes longer than usual because of a right whale Slow Zone that stays in place through that last sailing 17. City Cruises' Provincetown Fast Ferry runs seasonally, mid-May through mid-October 18, and Captain John Boats' Plymouth–Provincetown ferry is closed for the season 19.

Whale watching. Hyannis Whale Watcher Cruises' last trip of 2026 was Sunday, November 1 20, and Captain John Boats is closed until spring 19. Right whales can show up early, though: last season's first right whale was reported from Race Point Beach by a member of the public on October 26, 2025, and the first aerial survey of the season flew on November 3, 2025 45.

Our winter whales and seals guide covers where to look from shore and how close you can legally get.

Cold-stunned sea turtles. Mass Audubon says sea turtle strandings on Cape Cod mainly happen from November to mid-January, mostly along Cape Cod Bay 46. If you find one, call its Wellfleet Bay hotline at 508-349-2615, option 2 46.

Buses. CCRTA's FLEX (Harwich–Provincetown), H2O (Hyannis–Orleans) and Sealine (Hyannis–Falmouth–Woods Hole) routes run Monday through Saturday on the schedule for September 8, 2026, to January 22, 2027 212223. All three run a Saturday schedule on Veterans Day, November 11, and have no service on Thanksgiving 212223. The Provincetown–North Truro Shuttle ran through October 11, 2026 24, and the Hyannis Trolley is a summer service, Memorial Day to Labor Day 25. CCRTA's home page advertises free fares on all fixed routes 26.

Driving over? Check live Sagamore and Bourne bridge traffic before you leave, especially on Thanksgiving weekend.

Museums, cranberries and markets

  • Cape Cod Museum of Natural History, Brewster: fall 2026 hours are Wednesday through Sunday, 10 AM to 3 PM, with last admission at 2:30 PM; it's closed on Veterans Day, November 11, and Thanksgiving Day, November 26 28.
  • Heritage Museums & Gardens, Sandwich: now closed for its main season 29. It reopens for Gardens Aglow, 4:30 to 8:30 PM, starting Friday, November 27 50; all dates are in our holiday events guide.
  • Cranberries: the Cape Cod Cranberry Growers' Association says the harvest runs once a year from mid-September through early November 27.
  • Orleans Farmers' Market: outdoors every Saturday from 9 AM to noon at 19 Old Colony Way through November 21 30. It's year-round, but it takes a break the week after Thanksgiving; from December through April it moves inside to 5 Namskaket Road, 10 AM to noon 30. It has also moved inside to 5 Namskaket Road in wet weather this fall, so check its site before you go 30.
  • Falmouth Farmers Market: Sundays from 10 AM to 1 PM in the Mullen Hall School parking lot, through November 15, 2026 31.

Thanksgiving week: November 25–29

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 28.

  • Chatham Turkey Trot: a 5K run/walk on Thanksgiving morning to benefit the Lower Cape Outreach Council 42, starting at 8 AM 42 from the Oyster Pond parking lot, 80 Stage Harbor Road 41. Registration is $45 per person from November 7 through race day; the online price is $48.70 including a $3.70 fee 42.
  • Thanksgiving Sandwich 5K: 8 AM at the Stop & Shop Plaza, 65 Route 6A, Sandwich; online registration closes at 6:30 AM race day, and proceeds benefit the Sandwich and Bourne food pantries and the Cape Cod Military Support Foundation 43.
  • Provincetown: on Thanksgiving Day most businesses are closed, though some restaurants serve Thanksgiving menus; Friday through Sunday, shops, galleries and restaurants are open with special sales 40. Friday, November 27, 2026, is Pink Friday, a day for the town's small shops and galleries 44, and The Canteen's holiday market runs every weekend from the day after Thanksgiving to New Year's 47.
  • Closed on Thanksgiving: the Cape Cod Museum of Natural History 28; CCRTA's FLEX, H2O and Sealine buses don't run 212223.

In Provincetown, the holiday starts on Wednesday evening with the lighting of the Pilgrim Monument 40, and Gardens Aglow opens in Sandwich on Friday, November 27 50.

Quick answers

What is the weather like on Cape Cod in November?

NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals for Hyannis (Barnstable Municipal Airport) give November an average high of 53.5°F, an average low of 37.3°F and 4.13 inches of precipitation 1.

What time does it get dark on Cape Cod in November?

Early. Daylight saving time ended on November 1, 2026 5. The U.S. Naval Observatory's data for Hyannis puts sunset at 4:36 PM on November 1, 4:21 PM on November 15 and 4:12 PM on November 30, 2026 234.

Are Cape Cod beaches open in November?

Cape Cod National Seashore beaches such as Head of the Meadow and Herring Cove are listed as open, but beach entrance fee collection has ended for the season, and restrooms and showers are closed, though the vault toilets in those two beaches' parking lots are open year-round 8. Lifeguards are typically on duty at those beaches only from late June through Labor Day 10.

Do the ferries to Nantucket and Martha's Vineyard run in November?

Yes. The Steamship Authority runs daily, year-round service to both islands 14, and Hy-Line Cruises runs year-round Hyannis–Nantucket service 16. Hy-Line's Hyannis–Martha's Vineyard ferry has ended for the season 15.

Can you go whale watching on Cape Cod in November?

Hyannis Whale Watcher Cruises' last trip of 2026 was Sunday, November 1 20, and Captain John Boats in Plymouth is closed for the season 19. Last year, the first right whale of the 2025/26 season was reported from Race Point Beach by a member of the public on October 26, 2025 45.

What is open on Cape Cod on Thanksgiving?

It varies by business, so check ahead. In Provincetown, the chamber of commerce says most businesses are closed on Thursday, though some restaurants serve Thanksgiving menus, and shops, galleries and restaurants are open Friday through Sunday 40. The Cape Cod Museum of Natural History is closed on Thanksgiving Day 28.
