Cape Cod Holiday Events 2026: Strolls, Parades, Lights and Markets

2026 dates for Cape Cod Christmas strolls, parades and tree lightings by town: Chatham, Falmouth, Hyannis, Sandwich, Holly Folly and more, with organizer links.

By Versel JohnsonPublished October 26, 2026 · Updated November 8, 2026How we fact-check

Village strolls, lighted parades, Santa arriving by boat or fire truck, holiday markets and a garden full of lights: here are the 2026 dates Cape Cod organizers have announced so far, town by town and weekend by weekend, each linked to its source. A few haven't posted 2026 dates yet; they're at the end, and we'll update this guide as organizers announce.

Season openers: mid-November through Thanksgiving weekend

Glassblower's Christmas, Sandwich Glass Museum (November 13–December 30). The museum's Glassblower's Christmas 2026 runs November 13 through December 30, with live glassblowing demonstrations hourly, on the hour [32].

Holidays at Highfield, Falmouth (November 19–December 20). Highfield Hall & Gardens is open Thursday through Sunday from 10 AM to 4 PM for Holidays at Highfield 2026, and closed Thanksgiving Day [29]. This year's "'Twas the Night Before Christmas: Life Above & Below Stairs" takes visitors through the final hours before Christmas morning in the Gilded Age home [29]. Admission is $20 for members and $25 for non-members, free for children under 17 and military, and Friday Twilight Hours run from 4:30 to 6 PM for $35 per adult [29].

The Polar Express Train Ride, Buzzards Bay (from November 20). Cape Cod Central Railroad's Polar Express page said "Tickets for 2026 now available!" when we checked on November 8, 2026. Trains depart from 70 Main Street in Buzzards Bay, and the ticket page lists an opening-weekend fare valid 11/20–11/22 [9].

Pilgrim Monument lighting, Provincetown (Wednesday, November 25). The Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum's Annual Lighting On The Hill runs from 4 to 5:30 PM on Thanksgiving Eve; at 5 PM the crowd counts down and the monument is lit for the season, and the event is free [20]. Some other Provincetown calendars list a different start time, so we've gone with the museum's own listing.

Gardens Aglow at Heritage Museums & Gardens, Sandwich (from November 27). Gardens Aglow 2026 runs November 27–29, December 2–6, December 8–13, December 15–23 and December 26–27, from 4:30 to 8:30 PM nightly with last entry at 8 PM [1]. General admission for non-members is $30 for adults and $20 for youth ages 3–17; children 2 and under are free but need a reservation, and Heritage strongly advises buying online in advance because walk-up sales aren't guaranteed [1].

Spectacle of Trees, JFK Hyannis Museum (from November 27). The museum is open for its Spectacle of Trees on November 27 and 28 from 10 AM to 6 PM, on Sunday, November 29, from noon to 6 PM, and on December 3–6 and 10–12 from 10 AM to 6 PM [31].

The Cape Cod Christmas Market, Mashpee (November 27–29). A three-day holiday market at the Naukabout Beer Garden, 4 Job's Fishing Road, open 11 AM to 2 PM on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, November 27–29. Admission is free, and the organizers promise handcrafted gifts from local artisans, festive food and warm drinks, live entertainment, visits with Santa and an annual community food drive [2].

Brewster for the Holidays (November 27–December 23). The Brewster Chamber of Commerce's town-wide celebration runs from Thanksgiving weekend to just before Christmas; its main events are on the first weekend of December, below [5].

Lobster Pot Tree lighting, Provincetown (Saturday, November 28, 5–6 PM). The Provincetown Chamber of Commerce hosts the lighting of the Fisherman's Christmas Tree, made from lobster pots, at the foot of MacMillan Pier next to its visitor center [21].

First weekend of December: December 4–6

The biggest cluster of 2026 events we found lands on this weekend.

Falmouth Holidays by the Sea (December 4–6). The Falmouth Chamber of Commerce has posted a tentative 2026 schedule that it says is subject to change, and asks people to call 508-548-8500 to confirm. Its events are held rain or shine [3]:

  • Friday, Dec. 4, 5–8 PM: Nobska Lighthouse Holiday Open House. Arrival is by trolley only, with no public parking, and the trolley stop is at the Candle House, 127 Water Street; caroling in Woods Hole village starts at 5:30 PM [3].
  • Saturday, Dec. 5, 10:30 AM: Seafaring Santa arrives by boat at Falmouth Harbor [3].
  • Saturday, Dec. 5, 3 PM: the 15th annual GhELF Run, followed by the 15th annual Jingle Jog 5K [3].
  • Saturday, Dec. 5, 4–8 PM: the Falmouth Village Holiday Stroll on Main Street and Queen's Buy Way, hosted by the Falmouth Village Association, with Santa at Barbo's Furniture and parking at the Mullen-Hall and Lawrence schools; the Village Green is lit at 6 PM [3].
  • Sunday, Dec. 6, noon: the 63rd annual Falmouth Chamber of Commerce Christmas Parade, themed "From Sea to Shining Sea: Celebrating 250 Years of America," stepping off from Dillingham Avenue and Davis Straits and running along Main Street to the Village Green [3].

Hyannis Holiday (December 4–5). Arts Barnstable, the Greater Hyannis Civic Association, the Hyannis Main Street BID and Love Live Local run a joint holiday weekend downtown, and its website lists Friday, December 4, and Saturday, December 5, 2026 [11]. The Hyannis Main Street Business Improvement District lists a Sip & Shop on Friday from 5 to 8 PM [13] and Hyannis Holiday on Saturday at noon [12], and Love Live Local holds its Love Local Fest on Saturday from 11 AM to 4 PM at 367 Main Street [14]. The weekend's website also lists a Main Street Stroll on Saturday from 11 AM to 3 PM, with Santa arriving by antique fire truck, and a Harbor Boat Parade from 4 to 6 PM in which Santa arrives by boat, but parts of that page are still 2025 text, so confirm times closer to the date [11].

Christmas in Harwich (Friday, December 4). The Harwich Chamber of Commerce's Friday night schedule [16]:

  • 5:30 PM: the annual tree lighting and caroling in Doane Park, and the start of the Harwich Port Stroll through Harwich Port's shops and galleries [16].
  • 5–8 PM: festive trolleys loop through Harwich Port and Harwich Center, starting at the chamber [16].
  • 6 PM: trees light up in each of Harwich's seven villages for the Seven Trees for Seven Villages tradition [16].
  • 6:15 PM: Santa arrives at the chamber's porch on a Harwich fire truck, with visits until 8 PM [16].
  • 6–8 PM: a Harwich Town Band concert at TD Bank [16].

Brewster for the Holidays (December 4–6). The Brewster Chamber of Commerce's 2026 main events [5]:

  • Friday, Dec. 4, 4 PM: the Christmas Tree and Windmill Lighting at Drummer Boy Park, with Santa arriving on the Brewster Fire Truck [5].
  • Friday, Dec. 4, 7 PM: the Brewster Band's annual holiday concert at Brewster Baptist Church, 1848 Main Street. It's free, with a free-will offering for Food4Kids [5].
  • Saturday, Dec. 5, 8:30 and 10:30 AM: the Brewster Recreation Department's Breakfast with Santa at Ocean Edge Resort & Golf Club, 2907 Main Street. It's $12 per person, children 2 and under are free, and seating is limited, so register ahead [5].
  • Sunday, Dec. 6, 11 AM–1 PM: meet Santa at the Cape Cod Museum of Natural History; admission for the event is free [5].

Wellfleet Tree Lighting & Sing-Along (Friday, December 4). On the Town Hall Green, the Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce starts with a short tree-lighting ceremony followed by caroling led by local musicians, including carolers from Wellfleet Elementary School, with free hot cocoa and goody bags for kids while supplies last [18]. The chamber's page doesn't list a start time yet [18].

Wequassett Resort, Harwich (December 4–5). The resort's tree lighting is Friday, December 4, at 4:30 PM at its Main Fountain, with Christmas lights, carols and hot cocoa [22]. On Saturday, December 5, its annual Merry Market runs from 1 to 5 PM, Santa arrives at 3 PM, and fireworks go up over Pleasant Bay at 5:15 PM [6]. The resort is at 2173 Head of the Bay Road (Route 28), and extra parking is available at Lighthouse Charter School in Harwich, with shuttles from 2 to 8 PM [6].

Holly Folly, Provincetown (opens December 4). The Provincetown Business Guild's 29th annual Holly Folly runs all four weekends in December: December 4–6, 11–13, 18–20 and 25–27 [19]. The Provincetown Chamber of Commerce lists the kickoff weekend as Friday through Sunday, December 4–6, 2026 [4]. Opening-weekend highlights include the Holly Dolly Folly Follies holiday variety show on Friday at 8 PM, the Jingle Bell Fun Run & Brunch Fundraiser on Saturday at 10 AM, and Holigays! with the Boston Gay Men's Chorus on Saturday at 8 PM [19].

Cape Symphony's Holiday on the Cape, Hyannis (December 4–6). Six performances at the Barnstable Performing Arts Center, 744 West Main Street: Friday at 4 and 8 PM, Saturday at 3 and 7:30 PM, and Sunday at 1 and 5 PM [30].

Santa Stampede 5K, Orleans (Saturday, December 5). A holiday family fun run from The Barley Neck Inn to Nauset Beach and back, with registration at 8:30 AM and the start at 10 AM; runners of all ages are encouraged to dress in their holiday best [33].

Christmas in Cotuit (Saturday, December 5). The Cotuit-Santuit Civic Association lists Christmas in Cotuit at 3:45 PM at the Town Dock, followed by refreshments and fun at Memorial Park [26]. In 2025, that 3:45 PM moment was Santa arriving by boat, followed by the tree lighting [26].

Centerville's Christmas Stroll Along Main Street (Sunday, December 6, 4–6 PM). The Centerville Civic Association says Main Street residents bring musical serenades, dance performances, crafts and food, and Santa greets children at his own workshop; performance details are coming closer to the stroll [25].

Yarmouth Port Christmas Stroll (Sunday, December 6, 1–4 PM). The Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce and the Historical Society of Old Yarmouth host this free stroll along Route 6A, and the annual tree lighting is at 4:15 PM [7]. The chamber describes complimentary trolley rides, live holiday music, crafts, photos with Santa and a winter petting zoo [7].

Second weekend of December: December 11–13

Chatham's Christmas by the Sea (December 11–13). The Chatham Chamber of Commerce lists a Friday night Christmas stroll and tree lighting on Main Street, Breakfast with Santa at the Wayside Inn on Saturday morning, and live entertainment and activities around town on Saturday and Sunday [15]. Times aren't posted yet, and the chamber says to check back for more detailed weekend activities [15].

Osterville Christmas Stroll & Open House (from Friday, December 11). The Osterville Business and Professional Association's calendar lists its Christmas Stroll & Open House starting Friday, December 11, in Osterville Village at Main Street and Wianno Avenue [27]. The association describes a festive Friday night when the village glows with twinkling lights, carolers and open-late shops [28].

Mashpee Holiday Stroll and Season of Illumination Parade (Saturday, December 12). Mashpee Commons lists the Mashpee Holiday Stroll followed by the Mashpee Chamber Parade on Saturday, December 12, from 1 to 7:30 PM at Central Square, 22 Steeple Street [23]. The Mashpee Chamber of Commerce's listing for its 20th annual Season of Illumination Parade, co-hosted with Mashpee Commons, shows a 6:30 PM start while its description says 6:00 PM, so arrive early [8].

Sandwich Town-Wide Stroll (Sunday, December 13, 11 AM–4 PM). This year the Sandwich Chamber of Commerce is expanding its annual Village Stroll into a town-wide event, inviting businesses in the Village, East Sandwich and South Sandwich to host holiday cheer, live entertainment, giveaways and special shopping [24].

Holly Folly's second weekend, Provincetown (December 11–13). The weekend includes Bingo Crosby on Sunday, December 13, at 1 PM [19].

Later in December

  • Harwich's Trolley to Christmas Town: Saturday and Sunday, December 19–20, with tickets sold through the Harwich Chamber of Commerce [17].
  • Holly Folly's last two weekends: December 18–20 and 25–27 in Provincetown [19].
  • Last chances: Holidays at Highfield runs through December 20 [29], Brewster for the Holidays through December 23 [5], Gardens Aglow through December 27 [1] and Glassblower's Christmas through December 30 [32].

Quick answers

When is the Falmouth Christmas stroll in 2026?

The Falmouth Village Holiday Stroll is Saturday, December 5, 2026, from 4 to 8 PM on Main Street and Queen's Buy Way, with the lighting of the Village Green at 6 PM, according to the Falmouth Chamber of Commerce's tentative 2026 schedule, which it says is subject to change [3].

When is the Falmouth Christmas parade in 2026?

Sunday, December 6, 2026, at noon, according to the Falmouth Chamber of Commerce's tentative 2026 schedule. The 63rd annual parade steps off from Dillingham Avenue and Davis Straits and heads along Main Street to the Village Green [3].

When is the Chatham Christmas stroll in 2026?

Chatham's Christmas by the Sea weekend is Friday through Sunday, December 11–13, 2026, starting with a Friday night Christmas stroll and tree lighting on Main Street, according to the Chatham Chamber of Commerce [15].

When is the Hyannis Christmas stroll in 2026?

The organizers' Hyannis Holiday website lists the weekend as Friday and Saturday, December 4–5, 2026, with the Main Street Stroll on Saturday from 11 AM to 3 PM, but parts of that page still carry 2025 text, so confirm times closer to the date [11]. The Hyannis Main Street Business Improvement District lists Hyannis Holiday on Saturday, December 5, at noon [12].

When is the Mashpee Christmas parade in 2026?

Saturday, December 12, 2026. Mashpee Commons lists the Mashpee Holiday Stroll followed by the Mashpee Chamber Parade that day from 1 to 7:30 PM at Central Square, 22 Steeple Street [23].

When is the Sandwich Christmas stroll in 2026?

Sunday, December 13, 2026, from 11 AM to 4 PM. The Sandwich Chamber of Commerce is expanding its annual Village Stroll into a town-wide event this year [24].

When is the Harwich Port Christmas stroll in 2026?

Friday, December 4, 2026. The Harwich Chamber of Commerce's schedule starts the Harwich Port Stroll at 5:30 PM, the same time as the tree lighting and caroling in Doane Park, and Santa arrives by fire truck at 6:15 PM [16].

When is Holly Folly 2026 in Provincetown?

The 29th annual Holly Folly runs all four weekends in December 2026: December 4–6, 11–13, 18–20 and 25–27, according to the Provincetown Business Guild [19].

When is the Pilgrim Monument lighting in 2026?

Wednesday, November 25, 2026, Thanksgiving Eve. The Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum's Annual Lighting On The Hill runs from 4 to 5:30 PM, the monument is lit at 5 PM, and the event is free [20].

When does Gardens Aglow 2026 start?

Gardens Aglow 2026 at Heritage Museums & Gardens in Sandwich opens November 27 and runs on select nights through December 27, from 4:30 to 8:30 PM with last entry at 8 PM [1].

Is the Polar Express train running on Cape Cod in 2026?

Yes. When we checked on November 8, 2026, Cape Cod Central Railroad's Polar Express page said "Tickets for 2026 now available!" The train departs from 70 Main Street in Buzzards Bay, and the ticket page lists an opening-weekend fare valid 11/20–11/22 [9].
