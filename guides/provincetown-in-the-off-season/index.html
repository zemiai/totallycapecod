Provincetown in the Off-Season: What's Open and What to Do

Provincetown in winter 2026–27: what's open, ferry and Cape Air schedules, parking, Pilgrim Monument and PAAM hours, Seashore trails and dated events.

Provincetown doesn't shut down after the summer, but it does shrink. Some restaurants close for the season while others stay open daily or on set days 1, and the Boston and Plymouth ferries don't run again until spring 2829.

This guide covers what's confirmed open, how to get there without a ferry, and the dated events between now and late April 2027.

What's open in Provincetown this winter

The Provincetown Business Guild keeps a What's Open page, last updated November 19, 2026 1. It's organized by category, from restaurants and cafes, bars, retail and galleries to attractions, transportation, services and lodging, and many listings note the days a business is open, year-round status or a seasonal closure 1. The guild recommends checking each business's website or social media to reconfirm hours 1.

Thanksgiving week. On Thanksgiving Day most Provincetown businesses are closed, but some restaurants open with special Thanksgiving menus, according to the Provincetown Chamber of Commerce 40. Getting there: no ferries until May, but Cape Air flies

Ferries are done for 2026. Bay State Cruise Company's 2026 Boston–Provincetown season has concluded; its daily service ran through October 18 and weekend service through November 15, and it says its 2027 season begins May 17 28. Capt. John Boats' Plymouth–Provincetown fast ferry is closed for the season, with "see you in the spring" and no 2027 date posted 29. The Provincetown Business Guild lists Boston Harbor City Cruises as closed for the season and resuming May 15, 2027 1.

Flights. Cape Air offers four round-trip flights a week between Boston and Provincetown this winter, from November 4, 2026, through May 12, 2027, according to the town's airport page 30:

Route Departs
Boston to Provincetown 4:30 p.m. Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 30
Provincetown to Boston 7:30 a.m. Thursday, Friday and Saturday; 1:30 p.m. Sunday 30

The fare is $109 one-way, including taxes and fees 30.

Bus. CCRTA's FLEX route serves Harwich, Brewster, Orleans, Eastham, Wellfleet, Truro and Provincetown 31. Its current schedule runs September 8, 2026, to January 22, 2027, Monday through Saturday, and rides on all CCRTA fixed routes are free 31. There's no FLEX service on Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 18) runs on the Saturday schedule 31. FLEX can go up to ¾ mile off route by reservation if you book at least 2 hours ahead 31. The Provincetown–North Truro Shuttle's season ended October 11, 2026, and is listed to resume in spring 2027 32.

Driving. Check live Sagamore and Bourne bridge delays before you leave.

Parking in the off-season

The town's parking page says the paid parking season ends Monday, November 2, 2026, and resumes May 1, 2027 33. During the paid season, the town lists the MacMillan Pier lot at $4 an hour ($40 daily maximum) and the Grace Hall lot, at Bradford and Prince streets, at $3 an hour ($30 daily maximum) 33. The MacMillan Pier lot has 323 spaces and Grace Hall has 354, and both are open 24 hours a day 34. The town's pages don't spell out off-season charges, so check the signs at the lot.

Under the town's parking regulations, Emergency Snow Routes are posted in winter as tow-away zones with no parking in the event of snowfall 35. If snow is in the forecast, don't leave your car on a posted route.

Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum

The monument and museum are open Friday through Sunday and closed for the season starting December 6 2. Hours are 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., with the last ticket sold at 3 p.m. and the last climb at 3:15 p.m. 2. Admission is $23 for adults; $18 for teens 13–17, seniors 65 and over and veterans; $10 for youth 4–12; and free for kids 3 and under 2. Tickets are sold onsite during open hours 2.

Lighting on the Hill (Wednesday, November 25). The museum's annual lighting runs from 4 to 5:30 p.m. on Thanksgiving Eve, with the countdown and "throw the switch" at 5 p.m. to light the monument for the season 3. The lighting is the museum's one annual fundraiser 3.

The museum hasn't posted a 2027 reopening date that we could find.

Cape Cod National Seashore in winter

Here's the National Park Service's current status for the Seashore in Provincetown:

  • Province Lands Visitor Center: the visitor center and its park store have closed for the season, but restrooms in the parking area are open year-round 13. The Park Service says the outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting, and that whales and seabirds are often spotted from there 14.
  • Race Point Beach: open; its vault toilets and showers are closed 13.
  • Herring Cove Beach: open, with vault toilets in the parking lot open year-round; the bathhouse restrooms and showers are closed 13. The Park Service calls it a popular place to watch the sunset 23.
  • Fees: beach entrance fee collection and annual pass sales have ended for the season 13. Entrance fees are only required seasonally at park beaches, and the park doesn't accept cash 15.
  • Driving on the beach: the oversand (ORV) corridor is in its Limited Access off-season, and both the corridor and the Oversand Permit Office reopen in April 2027 13. During Limited Access, only Annual ORV Permit holders can use the corridor, between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m., and never within 2 hours before or after high tide 16.

Trails. The Seashore's 11 walking trails are open year-round 17, and all three bike trails are open now 13.

  • Beech Forest Trail: a 1-mile loop at 36 Race Point Road 18. Pets aren't permitted on most Seashore walking trails 13.
  • Province Lands Bike Trail: a 5.45-mile loop with spurs to Herring Cove, Race Point Beach and Bennett Pond, rated advanced-intermediate for its steep hills, sharp turns and tunnels 19. Leashed pets are allowed on it from November 1 through April 30 13.

Lighthouses. Race Point, Wood End and Long Point lights can only be reached by walking several miles through sand; Wood End and Long Point aren't open to the public, and the American Lighthouse Foundation's Cape Cod chapter, which operates Race Point Light, occasionally hosts open houses 21. For more winter ideas across the Cape, see our things to do on Cape Cod in winter guide.

What's seasonal. The Old Harbor Life-Saving Station museum at Race Point is brought to life by volunteers during summer open houses 20, and ranger and volunteer-guided programs are generally offered from May through October 24.

Hunting season. Hunting in the Seashore generally follows Massachusetts seasons, and hunting or carrying a loaded weapon is prohibited on or within 500 feet of established bike and nature trails 22.

Whales, seals and winter birds

Right whales typically arrive in Cape Cod Bay in early winter, and many stay through mid-May, according to the Center for Coastal Studies in Provincetown 25. Our whales and seals in winter guide covers where to watch from shore and how close the law lets you get.

  • Winter Birding: Ducks, Loons & Grebes: Mass Audubon's Wellfleet Bay sanctuary leads this outdoor program at MacMillan Wharf in Provincetown on Saturday, January 2, 2027, from 9 to 10 a.m. 27.
  • Center for Coastal Studies: its Turkey Trash Trek beach cleanup is Saturday, November 28, 2026, at a location to be determined, though the center notes the date may change 26. Its Holly Folly Spinnaker Talk & Craft Fair is Saturday, December 5, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at 5 Holway Avenue 26.

Planning tips

  • Check hours twice. Use the Business Guild's What's Open list, then confirm with the business itself 1.
  • Plan around Cape Air's days. Winter flights from Boston leave Wednesday through Saturday only 30.
  • Bring layers. Weather on the Outer Cape can change quickly; see our Cape Cod in December guide for typical conditions.

Quick answers

Does the Provincetown ferry run in winter?

No. Bay State Cruise Company's 2026 Boston–Provincetown season has concluded, and it says its 2027 season begins May 17 28. Capt. John Boats' Plymouth–Provincetown fast ferry is closed for the season 29. The Provincetown Business Guild lists Boston Harbor City Cruises as closed for the season, resuming May 15, 2027 1.

How do you get to Provincetown in winter without a car?

Cape Air flies four round trips a week between Boston and Provincetown from November 4, 2026, through May 12, 2027, at $109 one-way including taxes and fees 30. On the Cape, CCRTA's FLEX bus serves Harwich to Provincetown Monday through Saturday on its September 8, 2026–January 22, 2027 schedule, and rides on CCRTA fixed routes are free 31.

What restaurants are open in Provincetown in winter?

The Provincetown Business Guild keeps a What's Open list, last updated November 19, 2026 1. It lists Bay Cafe as open 365 days a year, and Kohi Coffee, Liz's Cafe/Anybody's Bar and Mac's Fish House as open daily, while the Lobster Pot and Pepe's Wharf are closed for the season 1. The guild recommends reconfirming hours on each business's website or social media 1.

Is the Pilgrim Monument open in winter?

Only into early December. The Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum is open Friday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., and closed for the season starting December 6 2.

Do you have to pay for parking in Provincetown in winter?

The town's parking page says the paid parking season ends Monday, November 2, 2026, and resumes May 1, 2027 33; during the paid season, the MacMillan Pier lot is $4 an hour and the Grace Hall lot $3 an hour 33. The town's pages don't spell out off-season charges, so check the signs at the lot. On New Year's Eve, the town says free parking will be available at the MacMillan Pier lot and throughout town 36. Under town regulations, posted Emergency Snow Routes are tow-away zones with no parking in the event of snowfall 35.

What is there to do in Provincetown on New Year's Eve?

The town's First Light drone show is Thursday, December 31, 2026, at approximately 6 p.m. over Provincetown Harbor, with the MacMillan Pier lot as the main viewing area and free parking there and throughout town 36. The First Light celebration runs December 31, 2026, to January 3, 2027 37, and the Provincetown Polar Bear Plunge is Friday, January 1, 2027 43.

Are Cape Cod National Seashore beaches open in Provincetown in winter?

Yes. Race Point Beach is open, though its vault toilets and showers are closed, and Herring Cove Beach is open with year-round vault toilets in the parking lot 13. Beach entrance fee collection has ended for the season 13.
