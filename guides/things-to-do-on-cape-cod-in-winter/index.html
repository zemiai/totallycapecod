Winter on Cape Cod means short days, cold air and a much quieter Cape. The National Seashore, wildlife sanctuaries, several museums and an indoor farmers market stay open, and there's a steady calendar of owl walks, concerts and theater. Here's what's open from December through February, and the winter events organizers have confirmed so far.

Cape Cod National Seashore in winter

The Seashore is open year-round, but from November through April some visitor facilities are closed 4. Here's what the Park Service lists for the off-season:

  • Salt Pond Visitor Center, Eastham: the Seashore's year-round visitor center, with a theater, museum, park store and restrooms 6. As of the Park Service's November 2026 conditions update, it's open 9 AM to 4:30 PM 5. Its satellite restroom building in the parking lot closes for work beginning December 1, with portable toilets available 5.
  • Province Lands Visitor Center, Provincetown: closed for the season 5; it's open May through October, but its outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting 6. Restrooms in its parking area are open year-round 5.
  • Trails: the Park Service says the Seashore's 11 walking trails are open year-round 7. Beginning November 30, a small stretch of the Nauset Marsh Trail around the bridge closes for about a month while staff replace the wooden planks 5.
  • Beaches: Coast Guard, Nauset Light, Marconi, Head of the Meadow, Race Point and Herring Cove beaches are listed as open, with beach entrance fee collection ended for the season 5. The vault toilets in the Head of the Meadow and Herring Cove parking lots are open year-round 5.
  • Dogs on bike trails: leashed pets are allowed on the Head of the Meadow Bike Trail year-round, and on the Nauset and Province Lands bike trails from November 1 through April 30 5.
  • What's not running: ranger- and volunteer-guided programs are generally offered from May through October 8, and Highland Light's shop and tours typically run from early May through the end of October 9. The oversand (ORV) corridor reopens in April 2027 5.

Sanctuaries, state parks and winter walks

  • Wellfleet Bay Wildlife Sanctuary (Mass Audubon), South Wellfleet: the sanctuary lists its nature center as open daily, 9 AM to 5 PM, and its 5 miles of trails as open daily, dawn to dusk 10. The temporary boardwalk to Try Island has been removed for the season, so Try Island and the Boardwalk Trail can be reached only at low tide 11.
  • Long Pasture Wildlife Sanctuary (Mass Audubon), Cummaquid: nature center Tuesday through Sunday, 9 AM to 3 PM, and 2.5 miles of trails open daily, dawn to dusk 12.
  • Monomoy National Wildlife Refuge, Chatham: the Morris Island part of the refuge is open seven days a week, year-round, from a half hour before sunrise to a half hour after sunset 13. Leashed dogs are allowed on designated trails from September 16 through April 30 13. The Interior Department's Wildland Fire Service plans an additional prescribed burn at the refuge in December 2026 (a first burn took place November 17), and access to all of South Monomoy Island, including the lighthouse, will be restricted while burning is in progress 13.
  • Nickerson State Park, Brewster: open sunrise to sunset 14. The 1,900-acre park has 8 freshwater ponds, 4 of them stocked with trout year-round, and an 8-mile bike path that connects to the Cape Cod Rail Trail 14.
  • Cape Cod Rail Trail: a paved trail through 6 Cape Cod towns, open sunrise to sunset 15.
  • Highfield Hall & Gardens, Falmouth: its grounds are open daily, year-round, dawn to dusk 29.

Winter wildlife

  • Right whales: they typically arrive in Cape Cod Bay in early winter, and many stay through mid-May, according to the Center for Coastal Studies 31.
  • Gray seal pups: females at colonies such as Monomoy Island give birth to a single white-coated pup from late December to mid-February 32.
  • Cold-stunned sea turtles: Mass Audubon says strandings mainly happen from November to mid-January 33. If you find one, don't put it back in the water; move it above the high tide line, cover it completely with seaweed, mark the spot and call the Wellfleet Bay hotline at 508-349-2615, option 2 33.

Museums open in winter

  • Cape Cod Museum of Art, Dennis: open 52 weeks a year, Tuesday through Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM and Sunday noon to 4 PM 20. Admission is $15 for adults, $12 for seniors 62 and older, and free for ages 21 and under 20. BothAnd, poetry by Brett Warren and art by Lauren Wolk, runs through February 28, 2027 21, and New Ground: Recent Acquisitions to the CCMoA Permanent Collection opens December 17, 2026 21.
  • JFK Hyannis Museum: from January 2027 through mid-April, open Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM 19. Adult admission is $14 19.
  • Provincetown Art Association and Museum (PAAM): open Wednesday through Sunday, noon to 5 PM, and on New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents' Day 22. General admission is $15, and youth 17 and under get in free 22. Members' Juried runs through January 17, 2027, and Recent Gifts to the Collection through January 31, 2027 23.
  • Cape Cod Maritime Museum, Hyannis: winter hours are Wednesday through Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM 24; adult admission is $10 24. Its winter talks include whale biology on January 7, marine chronometers on January 21, a 1944 Coast Guard surfboat on February 4 and the Gay Head Fresnel lens on February 18, 2027 24.
  • Sandwich Glass Museum: galleries are open by appointment only in January for annual maintenance 18. From February 1 through late March, it's open Wednesday through Sunday, 9:30 AM to 4 PM, and it's open on Washington's Birthday 18. Live glassblowing demonstrations run every hour on the hour; adult admission is $15 18.
  • Woods Hole Science Aquarium, Falmouth: run by NOAA Fisheries, it's open all year, Monday through Friday from 11 AM to 4 PM, closed weekends and federal holidays, with no admission charge 25. NOAA notes days and hours may vary 25.
  • Cape Cod Museum of Natural History, Brewster: when we checked on November 20, its hours page listed only fall 2026 hours, Wednesday through Sunday 10 AM to 3 PM, and closures December 21–26 and on New Year's Eve and Day 26. Check its site for winter hours.

Closed for the winter

  • WHOI Ocean Science Discovery Center, Woods Hole: closed November to mid-April 27.
  • Atlantic White Shark Conservancy's Shark Center, Chatham: closed for regular general admission for the season, apart from select holiday events, with an updated 2027 calendar still to come 28.
  • Highfield Hall galleries, Falmouth: the 2026 gallery season ran May 19 to October 25 29.
  • Province Lands Visitor Center and Highland Light: see the Seashore section above.

Public ice skating

  • Falmouth Ice Arena: public skating every Friday, 7:30 to 8:40 PM, through June 25, 2027, unless otherwise noted; $8 per person 16.
  • Charles Moore Arena, Orleans: public skating Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, plus Sundays (seasonally) from 1:30 to 3:30 PM; $10 admission and $4 skate rentals 17.

Rinks add and cancel sessions around hockey and holidays, so check the rink's page the day you go.

The winter farmers market

The Orleans Farmers' Market moves indoors every Saturday from December through April, 10 AM to noon, at 5 Namskaket Road, Orleans 30. It runs 50 weeks a year, taking a break the week after Thanksgiving and Christmas, and it's back outside on May 1, 2027 30.

Quick answers

Is Cape Cod National Seashore open in winter?

Yes. The Seashore is open year-round, though some visitor facilities close from November through April 4. Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham is the year-round visitor center 6, and the Park Service says its 11 walking trails are open year-round 7. The Province Lands Visitor Center in Provincetown is open only from May through October 6.

What museums are open on Cape Cod in winter?

The Cape Cod Museum of Art in Dennis is open 52 weeks a year 20. From January 2027 through mid-April, the JFK Hyannis Museum is open Thursday through Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM 19. PAAM in Provincetown is open Wednesday through Sunday, noon to 5 PM 22, and the Cape Cod Maritime Museum in Hyannis lists winter hours of Wednesday through Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM 24. The Sandwich Glass Museum's galleries are open by appointment only in January, then Wednesday through Sunday from February 1 through late March 18.

Is there public ice skating on Cape Cod?

Yes. Falmouth Ice Arena holds public skating every Friday from 7:30 to 8:40 PM through June 25, 2027, for $8 16. Charles Moore Arena in Orleans has public skating Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, plus Sundays (seasonally) from 1:30 to 3:30 PM, for $10, with $4 skate rentals 17.

Is there a winter farmers market on Cape Cod?

Yes. The Orleans Farmers' Market moves indoors every Saturday from December through April, 10 AM to noon, at 5 Namskaket Road in Orleans 30. It takes a break the week after Christmas 30.

How cold is Cape Cod in winter?

NOAA's 1991–2020 normals for Hyannis put the average January high at 39.9°F and low at 24.1°F, and the average February high at 41.0°F and low at 24.8°F 1.

Can you see seals and whales on Cape Cod in winter?

Right whales typically arrive in Cape Cod Bay in early winter, and many stay through mid-May, according to the Center for Coastal Studies 31. Gray seal females at colonies such as Monomoy Island give birth from late December to mid-February 32.
