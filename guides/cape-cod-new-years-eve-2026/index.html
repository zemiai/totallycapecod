New Year's Eve 2026 falls on a Thursday and New Year's Day 2027 on a Friday, which makes it a long weekend on Cape Cod. Here's what organizers have confirmed so far: a drone show and polar plunge in Provincetown, First Night Chatham's fireworks, Noon Year's Eve parties for kids, and First Day hikes and runs on January 1. Each item links to its source, and the things that haven't been announced yet are listed at the end.

First Night Chatham (Thursday, December 31)

First Night Chatham bills itself as a full day and evening of music, performance and celebration throughout town, with performances and activities from noon until 10 PM 4. December 31, 2026, will be its 36th New Year's Eve celebration: festivities start with the Town Photo at the Chatham Lighthouse at noon, followed by indoor and outdoor performances, the Noise Parade and fireworks, and more performances until 10 PM 4.

  • Town Photo, noon: on the Chatham Lighthouse grounds, button-free 5.
  • Noise Parade, 6 PM: it starts at the corner of Cross and Main, walks to the rotary and continues to Oyster Pond for the fireworks; it's button-free 5.
  • Fireworks, 6:30 PM: after the Noise Parade, at Oyster Pond, which is a new location this year, and button-free 5.

The organizer's activities page calls the 2026–27 lineup a preliminary schedule, subject to change 5.

Buttons. Adult buttons (17 and up) are $30, or $35 as of December 15; children's buttons (ages 3–16) are $5; and children 2 and under are free 6. All outdoor activities, and some indoor activities, are button-free 6.

Headquarters. First Night Chatham Headquarters is at 584 Main Street, open December 26–30 from 10 AM to 4 PM and on December 31 from 10 AM until 10 PM 7.

Provincetown's First Light (December 31–January 3)

The Provincetown Business Guild lists its First Light celebration from December 31, 2026, through January 3, 2027 2. Our Provincetown off-season guide covers what's open in town in winter.

Drone show, Thursday, December 31, about 6 PM. The town's First Light drone show takes place over Provincetown Harbor, a 300-drone display with custom designs and three-dimensional effects 1. The MacMillan Pier Municipal Parking Lot and the areas around it are the primary public viewing area, and free parking will be available there and throughout town 1. The town says businesses along Commercial Street will host viewing parties, with more details to be announced 1.

NYE Fros-Tea Dance, Thursday, December 31, 3–7 PM. The Business Guild's First Light calendar lists this party at The Canteen, 225 Commercial Street, closing out the Canteen's Holiday Market season 2.

Polar Bear Plunge, Friday, January 1, noon. Provincetown's 11th annual Polar Bear Plunge meets at the Johnson Street parking lot (14 Johnson Street), and at 12 PM everyone plunges into Provincetown Harbor 3. Registration opens at 11 AM, and all registrants must arrive by 11:30 AM 3. A single registration is $65 ($71.40 online, including RunSignup's $6.40 fee); teams of 2–5 register at the same price each, and each member commits to a personal fundraising goal of at least $500 3. Water shoes are required, because that part of the harbor has sharp rocks and oyster shells 3. Spectators can cheer from the Harbor Lounge viewing party, and fees and pledges support the Provincetown Business Guild 3.

Pilgrim Monument. The Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum's visit page says it's closed for the season starting December 6 28.

Noon Year's Eve and family events (Thursday, December 31)

Several Cape libraries count down to noon instead of midnight, and one has an afternoon New Year's craft:

  • Mashpee Public Library, 11 AM–noon: crafts, games, snacks and a countdown to noon in the Children's Program Room, for kids ages 6–11 13.
  • South Yarmouth Library, 11:30 AM–12:30 PM: a Noon Year's Eve party with crafts and snacks in the Leonard Room 14.
  • Centerville Public Library, 11 AM–noon: an interactive concert with Jonathan and Shelby Weir followed by a "Noon Year's Eve" countdown; it's free, with no registration 15.
  • East Falmouth Library, 2–3 PM: drop in to make a confetti popper; supplies are included and no registration is required 16.

New Year's Eve shows and parties

  • Cotuit Center for the Arts, Cotuit: three Scamps New Year's Eve comedy shows on Thursday, December 31, at 5, 7 and 9 PM, for ages 18 and up, with a cash bar 17. Advance tickets are $40, or $35 for seniors and members, and $45 at the door if seats remain 17.
  • LandShark Bar & Grill, Margaritaville Resort Cape Cod, Hyannis: live music from the David Garden Trio starts at 8:30 PM, with a complimentary champagne toast at midnight, and there are no tickets and no cover 18.
  • Four Points by Sheraton, Eastham: the Eastham Chamber of Commerce lists a New Year's Eve party on December 31, 2026, with comedy from Paul Nardizzi and Mike Donovan, a dinner buffet, and a DJ and dancing, for $99 per person including meals tax and gratuity 19. The listing doesn't give a start time 19.
  • Ocean Edge Resort, Brewster: a New Year's Eve prix fixe dinner is $140 per person, including a Moët & Chandon split, with reservations through OpenTable 20. The resort's holiday page also mentions a New Year's Day plunge into Cape Cod Bay, without a time or other details 20.

New Year's Day: plunges, a 5K and First Day hikes (Friday, January 1, 2027)

Yarmouth Polar Plunge, noon. Friends of Yarmouth Council on Aging lists its annual plunge into Nantucket Sound at Bass River Beach (Smugglers Beach) in South Yarmouth, benefiting the Yarmouth Senior Center, with the event running 11:30 AM–1 PM 12.

Sandwich First Day 5K, 11 AM. The 12th Annual First Day 5K starts from the Sandwich Recreation Department at Oak Crest Cove 11. Registration is $33.90 including fees, or $22.60 for ages 0–10 and 70–100 and $28.25 for ages 11–17 11.

Provincetown Polar Bear Plunge, noon. Details are above 3.

First Day Hikes Cape Cod. Barnstable Land Trust lists First Day Hikes Cape Cod on Friday, January 1, 2027, a one-day regional celebration with free walks, talks and hikes across the Cape 9. For scale, the 2026 edition represented 15 land conservation groups and included close to 20 special events 10.

Chatham Conservation Foundation First Day Hike, 10–11 AM. A free guided walk along the Barclay's Pond Trail, off Old Queen Anne Road in Chatham; registration is required 8.

The next day. Mass Audubon's Wellfleet Bay sanctuary leads Winter Birding: Ducks, Loons & Grebes at Macmillan Wharf in Provincetown on Saturday, January 2, 2027, from 9 to 10 AM, for $10 (members) or $12 (nonmembers) 21.

Cape Cod National Seashore. As of the park's November 16 update, Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham is open 9 AM to 4:30 PM, and Province Lands Visitor Center has closed for the season 22. A small stretch of the Nauset Marsh trail around the bridge is closed for about a month from November 30 while the planks are replaced 22. The page doesn't list New Year's Day hours, so check before you go.

Weather, buses and ferries

Weather. NOAA's 1991–2020 normals for Hyannis put the normal high at 41.8°F and the normal low at 25.8°F on December 31, and 41.6°F and 25.6°F on January 1 23. For the actual forecast, see our live conditions page or the app.

Buses. CCRTA has no fixed-route bus service on New Year's Day 24.

Ferries. The Steamship Authority's traditional ferry schedules for October 23, 2026, to January 5, 2027, mark trips as daily: from Woods Hole to Vineyard Haven the first departure is 6:00 AM 25, and from Hyannis to Nantucket the departures are 9:15 AM, 2:45 PM and 8:00 PM 26.

Driving. Heading over the canal for the long weekend? Check live Sagamore and Bourne bridge traffic before you leave.

Quick answers

What is there to do on Cape Cod on New Year's Eve 2026?

The two biggest public celebrations we found are First Night Chatham, its 36th New Year's Eve celebration, running from the noon Town Photo at Chatham Lighthouse until 10 PM 4, and Provincetown's First Light drone show over Provincetown Harbor at about 6 PM 1. Several libraries hold Noon Year's Eve countdowns for kids and families 131415, and the Cotuit Center for the Arts has comedy shows at 5, 7 and 9 PM 17.

Are there New Year's Eve fireworks on Cape Cod in 2026?

Yes, in Chatham. First Night Chatham's fireworks follow the Noise Parade at 6:30 PM at Oyster Pond, a new location this year, and they're button-free 5. Provincetown's New Year's Eve show is a 300-drone display over the harbor at about 6 PM 1.

When is the Provincetown New Year's Eve drone show?

Thursday, December 31, 2026, beginning at approximately 6 PM over Provincetown Harbor. The MacMillan Pier lot and the areas around it are the main public viewing area, with free parking there and throughout town 1.

How much are First Night Chatham buttons?

Adult buttons (17 and up) are $30, rising to $35 as of December 15; children's buttons (ages 3–16) are $5, and children 2 and under are free 6. All outdoor activities and some indoor ones are button-free 6.

Is there a polar plunge on Cape Cod on New Year's Day 2027?

Yes. Provincetown's 11th annual Polar Bear Plunge is at noon on Friday, January 1, 2027, from the 14 Johnson Street parking lot, with single registration at $65 ($71.40 including the online fee) 3. Yarmouth's Polar Plunge is at noon the same day at Bass River Beach (Smugglers Beach) in South Yarmouth, benefiting the Yarmouth Senior Center 12.

Are there First Day hikes on Cape Cod on January 1, 2027?

Yes. Barnstable Land Trust lists First Day Hikes Cape Cod on Friday, January 1, 2027, a one-day regional celebration of free walks, talks and hikes, though individual hikes weren't posted when we checked 9. The Chatham Conservation Foundation's free First Day Hike on the Barclay's Pond Trail runs from 10 to 11 AM, with registration required 8.

Do Cape Cod buses run on New Year's Day?

No. CCRTA's fall/winter 2026 riders guide says there is no service on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day 24.
