This is a town-by-town list of Cape Cod gift shops to check before your 2026 holiday shopping. It gives each shop’s address when we could confirm one, the hours the shop itself publishes, a phone number where we found one, and the proof we found that the shop is open. Every list has one person on it who has said they want nothing. Plan accordingly. <!-- nocite -->

## How this list was made

Shops come from the gift categories of town chambers of commerce. Where a chamber has no gift category, we used its general shopping list. The categories are:

- **Brewster:** Brewster Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [1]
- **Chatham:** Chatham Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [2]
- **Dennis:** Dennis Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [3]
- **Falmouth:** Falmouth Chamber of Commerce, “GIFTS & HOME ACCESSORIES” [4]
- **Hyannis:** Hyannis Area Chamber of Commerce, “Shops: Clothing, Home Furnishing, Gifts, Food” [5]
- **Mashpee:** Mashpee Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [6]
- **Orleans:** Orleans Chamber of Commerce, “Wedding Gifts” [7]
- **Provincetown:** Provincetown Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping” [8]
- **Sandwich:** Sandwich Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts, Clothing & Accessories” [9]
- **Truro:** Truro Chamber of Commerce, “Shop” [10]
- **Wellfleet:** Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [11]
- **Yarmouth:** Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [12]

Orleans uses a “Wedding Gifts” category. Mashpee, Truro and Yarmouth use general shopping lists, and Provincetown’s category is simply “Shopping.” <!-- nocite -->

We kept only shops with a walk-in storefront on the Cape. Each shop was checked on November 15, 2026, against its own website or its own social media pages. A chamber listing is not a recommendation, and the order here is alphabetical (by town, then by shop name, ignoring a leading “The”), not a ranking. <!-- nocite -->

Coverage is uneven. Harwich has no shop on the list and Yarmouth has one, because those chambers’ directories could not be read. Eastham and Bourne have none confirmed. <!-- nocite -->

Hours change, and many shops publish none. Where a shop gives hours without a date, we say so. Where a shop’s site still shows a spring or summer label, we do not repeat the hours. Confirm with the shop before you drive. <!-- nocite -->
