There's no single right month for a Cape Cod trip. It depends on whether you want warm water and lifeguards, quiet trails, a particular event or lighter bridge traffic. This guide puts the public numbers side by side, month by month, so you can pick for yourself: NOAA air and water temperatures, the National Park Service's visitor counts, daylight, what's open, and the 2027 event dates organizers have posted. <!-- nocite -->

Air temperatures are NOAA's 1991–2020 normals for Hyannis (Barnstable Municipal Airport) [1]. Water temperatures are monthly averages from NOAA's Coastal Water Temperature Guide for Woods Hole and for Chatham's Lydia Cove [2]. Visits are Cape Cod National Seashore recreation visits in 2025 [4].

## Summer: June through August

- **Busiest season.** The Park Service says the most popular months to visit the Seashore are June through September, and that in the busy summer season all roads and facilities are open with a full range of programs [9]. August 2025 was the Seashore's busiest month, with 672,062 recreation visits [4].
- **Lifeguards:** typically on duty at the six Seashore beaches from late June through Labor Day [9].
- **Beach fees:** Seashore beach entrance fees are collected in spring, summer and fall [10]; a private vehicle pass is $25 [10]. The annual pass covers the six beaches from late May through mid-September [10].
- **Bridge traffic:** see our [bridge traffic guide](/guides/sagamore-bourne-bridge-traffic-best-times/) for when the Sagamore and Bourne bridges back up. <!-- nocite -->

**2027 dates organizers have posted:** <!-- nocite -->

- **Provincetown Portuguese Festival:** June 25–27, 2027, ending with the Blessing of the Fleet on Sunday [18].
- **Falmouth fireworks:** Sunday, July 4, 2027, at dusk, around 9 PM; rain date Monday, July 5 [19].
- **Barnstable County Fair:** July 19–25, 2027, at the Cape Cod Fairgrounds [20].
- **Woods Hole Film Festival:** Saturday, July 31, to Saturday, August 7, 2027 [21].
- **Provincetown Carnival:** August 14–21, 2027, with the parade on Commercial Street on Thursday, August 19 [22].
- **Falmouth Road Race:** August 15, 2027 [23].
- **Chatham Band concerts:** in 2026 they were scheduled Friday nights at 8 PM from July 3 to September 4 [24]; the Chamber's listing covers the 2026 season only [24].

## Fall: September through November

- **Fewer visits, warm water early on.** The Seashore's recreation visits fell from 672,062 in August 2025 to 452,870 in September and 312,539 in October [4]. Water at Woods Hole averages 69.4°F in September, close to July's 71.2°F [2].
- **After Labor Day:** Seashore lifeguards typically finish at Labor Day [9], and the annual beach pass covers the beaches through mid-September [10].
- **Seasonal operations wind down.** Ranger- and volunteer-guided programs are generally offered from May through October [12], Highland Light's shop and tours typically run through the end of October [13], and the Province Lands Visitor Center is open May through October [11]. Hy-Line runs its Hyannis–Martha's Vineyard ferry seasonally, May through October [16].
- **Cape Cod Marathon Weekend:** October 9 and 10, 2027, celebrating 50 years [25].
- **Wellfleet OysterFest:** in 2026 it was Saturday and Sunday, October 17–18 [26]; the festival's FAQ lists only 2026 dates [26].

Our [Cape Cod in November guide](/guides/cape-cod-in-november/) covers late fall in detail. <!-- nocite -->

## Winter: December through March

- **Fewest visits.** In 2025 the Seashore recorded 115,743 recreation visits in January, 116,150 in February, 151,050 in March and 123,978 in December [4].
- **Still open:** the Seashore is open year-round, though from November through April some visitor facilities are closed [9]. Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham is the park's year-round visitor center [11].
- **Right whales:** they typically arrive in Cape Cod Bay in early winter, with many staying through mid-May, according to the Center for Coastal Studies [15].
- **Holidays:** for December strolls, parades and lightings, see our [holiday events guide](/guides/cape-cod-holiday-events-2026/). <!-- nocite -->

For what's open, owl walks, skating and winter concerts, see [things to do on Cape Cod in winter](/guides/things-to-do-on-cape-cod-in-winter/), [Cape Cod in December](/guides/cape-cod-in-december/) and our [whales and seals guide](/guides/whales-and-seals-cape-cod-winter/). <!-- nocite -->

## Spring: April and May

- **Visits climb.** The Seashore went from 151,050 recreation visits in March 2025 to 228,787 in April and 322,040 in May [4].
- **Cold water.** The water at Woods Hole averages 45.5°F in April and 54.8°F in May [2].
- **Whale watching starts.** Dolphin Fleet runs from April to October with multiple trips per day, and says it will reopen in April 2027 [14].
- **Services return:** the Province Lands Visitor Center reopens in May [11], ranger programs generally start in May [12], and Highland Light tours typically start in early May [13]. Bay State Cruise Company's Boston–Provincetown fast ferry begins its 2027 season on May 17 [17].

## Matching the month to the trip

These are planning notes drawn from the numbers above, not ratings: <!-- nocite -->

- **Swimming with lifeguards:** late June through Labor Day at Seashore beaches [9], when water at Woods Hole averages 64.0°F to 72.6°F from June to August [2].
- **Warm water with fewer people:** September, when water at Woods Hole averages 69.4°F [2] and Seashore visits were about two-thirds of August's in 2025 [4].
- **Fewest visitors:** January and February, with about 116,000 Seashore recreation visits each in 2025 [4].
