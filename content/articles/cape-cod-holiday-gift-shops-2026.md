faq:
  - q: "Which shops on this list use the words “daily” or “seven days” in their hours text?"
    a: "Four shops do, with caveats. The Brewster Book Store says “We are open 10 am-5 pm, 7 days a week!” [45], and its hours page lists “Upcoming Dates Open Close * November 26th Closed” [11]. The Brewster Store’s site says “We are open seven day a week year round.” [46], while its Instagram profile reads “Open 364 Days a Year!” [47]. Kandy Korner Gifts in Hyannis lists “Daily Hours - 10am - 6pm” [28]. HandCraft House Gallery in Brewster lists “Open Daily 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM” [53]. Bass River Mercantile in South Yarmouth does not use those words; its site says “General hours are 10-5 Monday through Sunday. On somedays we’re not here at all, but lately we’ve been here a lot….unless we’re not here.” [44]."
  - q: "Which shop on this list reopens on a stated date?"
    a: "Nut House Candle Company in Dennis Port. Its Instagram profile says “HOURS: CLOSED! Reopen November 27th” [22]."
  - q: "Which shops say they are closed on a holiday?"
    a: "FabVilla in Mashpee says “Closed- Easter Sunday, Thanksgiving, Christmas” [69]. The Brewster Book Store’s hours page lists “Upcoming Dates Open Close * November 26th Closed” [11]. The Cape Cod Museum of Natural History’s hours page lists “THANKSGIVING DAY THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 26TH” [50] and “WINTER HOLIDAYS MONDAY, DECEMBER 21ST - SATURDAY, DECEMBER 26TH” [50] under a “CLOSED” heading; those are museum closures, not confirmed for its shop."
  - q: "Does Truro Vineyards publish hours for after Christmas?"
    a: "Not on the visit page we read. The page says “We are OPEN YEAR ROUND Fall Hours (through just before Christmas) Monday-Saturday 11-5 Sunday 12-5” [82] and lists no hours for after Christmas."
  - q: "Which shops publish hours with a fall or other season label?"
    a: "Labelled fall: the Brewster Book Store’s “Fall Store Hours” table [11]; Truro Vineyards’ “Fall Hours (through just before Christmas)” [82]; DRIFT Wellfleet’s “Late Fall Hours” [83]; Masterson Made’s “Fall Hours” [63]; and the Cape Cod Museum of Natural History’s “Fall 2026 Hours”, which are museum hours [50]. Labelled by month: Stage Stop Candy’s “Showroom Hours: October - December” [62] and Provincetown Fudge Factory’s “NOVEMBER STORE HOURS” [37]. Ross Coppelman’s site still shows a “Spring Hours” label [61]. Adorn Cape Cod’s hours page carries “updated 9.13.26” [71] but no season label."

This is a town-by-town list of Cape Cod gift shops to check before your 2026 holiday shopping. It gives each shop’s address, the hours the shop itself publishes, a phone number where we found one, and the most recent activity we found on the shop’s own pages. We tend to begin a holiday list with confidence and finish it with a sense of surprise. <!-- nocite -->

## How this list was made

Shops come from the gift or shopping categories of town chambers of commerce. The categories we can cite are:

- **Brewster:** Brewster Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [1]
- **Chatham:** Chatham Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [2]
- **Dennis:** Dennis Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts & Specialty Items” [3]
- **Hyannis:** Greater Hyannis Chamber of Commerce, “Shops: Clothing, Home Furnishing, Gifts, Food” [4]
- **Orleans:** Orleans Chamber of Commerce, “Wedding Gifts” [5]
- **Provincetown:** Provincetown Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping” [6]
- **Sandwich:** Sandwich Chamber of Commerce, “Gifts, Clothing & Accessories” [7]
- **Truro:** Truro Chamber of Commerce, “Shop” [8]
- **Wellfleet:** Wellfleet Chamber of Commerce, “Gift Shops” [9]
- **Yarmouth:** Yarmouth Chamber of Commerce, “Shopping & Specialty Retail” [10]

Three shops, Celebrations of Cape Cod and Masterson Made in Falmouth and FabVilla in Mashpee, came from chamber listings (the Falmouth chamber’s guidebook and the Mashpee chamber’s member directory) that we could not confirm in a source we can cite, so no chamber listing is given for them. <!-- nocite -->

Each shop below has a street address on its own pages, and its entry gives that address with a citation. A chamber category is not a recommendation, and it does not promise a gift counter: where a page describes tastings, a museum or a studio visit, the entry says so. The order is alphabetical (by town, then by shop name, ignoring a leading “The”), not a ranking. Each shop was checked on November 15, 2026 for published hours, recent posts or a stated reopening date. <!-- nocite -->

This list has no Harwich shop and one Yarmouth shop because checking of those towns’ chamber listings was not finished by November 15, 2026. No shop in Eastham or Bourne was confirmed from its own pages. <!-- nocite -->

Hours change, and many shops publish none. Where a shop gives hours without a date, we say so. Where a shop’s site still shows a spring or summer label, we do not repeat the hours. Confirm with the shop before you drive. <!-- nocite -->
