Provincetown doesn't shut down after the summer, but it does shrink. Some restaurants close for the season while others stay open daily or on set days [1], and the Boston and Plymouth ferries don't run again until spring [28][29].

This guide covers what's confirmed open, how to get there without a ferry, and the dated events between now and late April 2027. <!-- nocite -->

## What's open in Provincetown this winter

The Provincetown Business Guild keeps a What's Open page, last updated November 19, 2026 [1]. It's organized by category, from restaurants and cafes, bars, retail and galleries to attractions, transportation, services and lodging, and many listings note the days a business is open, year-round status or a seasonal closure [1]. The guild recommends checking each business's website or social media to reconfirm hours [1].

## Getting there: no ferries until May, but Cape Air flies

**Ferries are done for 2026.** Bay State Cruise Company's 2026 Boston–Provincetown season has concluded; its daily service ran through October 18 and weekend service through November 15, and it says its 2027 season begins May 17 [28]. Capt. John Boats' Plymouth–Provincetown fast ferry is closed for the season, with "see you in the spring" and no 2027 date posted [29]. The Provincetown Business Guild lists Boston Harbor City Cruises as closed for the season and resuming May 15, 2027 [1].

**Flights.** Cape Air offers four round-trip flights a week between Boston and Provincetown this winter, from November 4, 2026, through May 12, 2027, according to the town's airport page [30]:

| Route | Departs |
|---|---|
| Boston to Provincetown | 4:30 p.m. Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday [30] |
| Provincetown to Boston | 7:30 a.m. Thursday, Friday and Saturday; 1:30 p.m. Sunday [30] |

The fare is $109 one-way, including taxes and fees [30].

**Bus.** CCRTA's FLEX route serves Harwich, Brewster, Orleans, Eastham, Wellfleet, Truro and Provincetown [31]. Its current schedule runs September 8, 2026, to January 22, 2027, Monday through Saturday, and rides on all CCRTA fixed routes are free [31]. There's no FLEX service on Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year's Day, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 18) runs on the Saturday schedule [31]. FLEX can go up to ¾ mile off route by reservation if you book at least 2 hours ahead [31]. The Provincetown–North Truro Shuttle's season ended October 11, 2026, and is listed to resume in spring 2027 [32].

**Driving.** Check [live Sagamore and Bourne bridge delays](/cape-cod-bridge-traffic.html) before you leave. <!-- nocite -->

## Parking in the off-season

The town's parking page says the paid parking season ends Monday, November 2, 2026, and resumes May 1, 2027 [33]. During the paid season, the town lists the MacMillan Pier lot at $4 an hour ($40 daily maximum) and the Grace Hall lot, at Bradford and Prince streets, at $3 an hour ($30 daily maximum) [33]. The MacMillan Pier lot has 323 spaces and Grace Hall has 354, and both are open 24 hours a day [34]. The town's pages don't spell out off-season charges, so check the signs at the lot. <!-- nocite -->

Under the town's parking regulations, Emergency Snow Routes are posted in winter as tow-away zones with no parking in the event of snowfall [35]. If snow is in the forecast, don't leave your car on a posted route. <!-- nocite -->

## Pilgrim Monument and Provincetown Museum

The monument and museum are open Friday through Sunday and closed for the season starting December 6 [2]. Hours are 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., with the last ticket sold at 3 p.m. and the last climb at 3:15 p.m. [2]. Admission is $23 for adults; $18 for teens 13–17, seniors 65 and over and veterans; $10 for youth 4–12; and free for kids 3 and under [2]. Tickets are sold onsite during open hours [2].

**Lighting on the Hill (Wednesday, November 25).** The museum's annual lighting runs from 4 to 5:30 p.m. on Thanksgiving Eve, with the countdown and "throw the switch" at 5 p.m. to light the monument for the season [3]. The lighting is the museum's one annual fundraiser [3].

The museum hasn't posted a 2027 reopening date that we could find. <!-- nocite -->

## Cape Cod National Seashore in winter

Here's the National Park Service's current status for the Seashore in Provincetown: <!-- nocite -->

- **Province Lands Visitor Center:** the visitor center and its park store have closed for the season, but restrooms in the parking area are open year-round [13]. The Park Service says the outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting, and that whales and seabirds are often spotted from there [14].
- **Race Point Beach:** open; its vault toilets and showers are closed [13].
- **Herring Cove Beach:** open, with vault toilets in the parking lot open year-round; the bathhouse restrooms and showers are closed [13]. The Park Service calls it a popular place to watch the sunset [23].
- **Fees:** beach entrance fee collection and annual pass sales have ended for the season [13]. Entrance fees are only required seasonally at park beaches, and the park doesn't accept cash [15].
- **Driving on the beach:** the oversand (ORV) corridor is in its Limited Access off-season, and both the corridor and the Oversand Permit Office reopen in April 2027 [13]. During Limited Access, only Annual ORV Permit holders can use the corridor, between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m., and never within 2 hours before or after high tide [16].

**Trails.** The Seashore's 11 walking trails are open year-round [17], and all three bike trails are open now [13].

- **Beech Forest Trail:** a 1-mile loop at 36 Race Point Road [18]. Pets aren't permitted on most Seashore walking trails [13].
- **Province Lands Bike Trail:** a 5.45-mile loop with spurs to Herring Cove, Race Point Beach and Bennett Pond, rated advanced-intermediate for its steep hills, sharp turns and tunnels [19]. Leashed pets are allowed on it from November 1 through April 30 [13].

**Lighthouses.** Race Point, Wood End and Long Point lights can only be reached by walking several miles through sand; Wood End and Long Point aren't open to the public, and the American Lighthouse Foundation's Cape Cod chapter, which operates Race Point Light, occasionally hosts open houses [21]. For more winter ideas across the Cape, see our [things to do on Cape Cod in winter guide](/guides/things-to-do-on-cape-cod-in-winter/). <!-- nocite -->

**What's seasonal.** The Old Harbor Life-Saving Station museum at Race Point is brought to life by volunteers during summer open houses [20], and ranger and volunteer-guided programs are generally offered from May through October [24].

**Hunting season.** Hunting in the Seashore generally follows Massachusetts seasons, and hunting or carrying a loaded weapon is prohibited on or within 500 feet of established bike and nature trails [22].

## Whales, seals and winter birds

Right whales typically arrive in Cape Cod Bay in early winter, and many stay through mid-May, according to the Center for Coastal Studies in Provincetown [25]. Our [whales and seals in winter guide](/guides/whales-and-seals-cape-cod-winter/) covers where to watch from shore and how close the law lets you get. <!-- nocite -->

- **Winter Birding: Ducks, Loons & Grebes:** Mass Audubon's Wellfleet Bay sanctuary leads this outdoor program at MacMillan Wharf in Provincetown on Saturday, January 2, 2027, from 9 to 10 a.m. [27].
- **Center for Coastal Studies:** its Turkey Trash Trek beach cleanup is Saturday, November 28, 2026, at a location to be determined, though the center notes the date may change [26]. Its Holly Folly Spinnaker Talk & Craft Fair is Saturday, December 5, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at 5 Holway Avenue [26].
