November is the Cape's quiet month, and the calendar shifts to concerts, theater, craft fairs and Thanksgiving. Here's what the weather typically does, what's still running, and the events organizers have confirmed for the rest of November 2026. <!-- nocite -->

## November weather on Cape Cod

NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals for Hyannis (Barnstable Municipal Airport) show how quickly the Cape cools through late fall [1]:

| Month | Average high | Average low | Precipitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| October | 63.4°F [1] | 45.7°F [1] | 4.33 in [1] |
| November | 53.5°F [1] | 37.3°F [1] | 4.13 in [1] |
| December | 45.2°F [1] | 29.3°F [1] | 4.68 in [1] |

November's normal mean temperature at Hyannis is 45.4°F [1].

**Storms.** The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs through November 30 [7]. The National Weather Service says nor'easters, named for their northeast winds along the coast, can happen any time of year but are most frequent and most violent between September and April [6].


**Ocean temperatures.** See today's readings on our [Cape Cod water temperature page](/cape-cod-water-temperature-today.html). <!-- nocite -->

## Daylight: plan for 4 PM sunsets

Daylight saving time ended on Sunday, November 1, 2026 [5], so evenings come early. U.S. Naval Observatory data for Hyannis (Eastern Standard Time):

| Date (2026) | Sunrise | Sunset |
|---|---|---|
| Sun, Nov 1 | 6:13 AM [2] | 4:36 PM [2] |
| Sun, Nov 15 | 6:30 AM [3] | 4:21 PM [3] |
| Mon, Nov 30 | 6:48 AM [4] | 4:12 PM [4] |

Civil twilight ends about half an hour after sunset: at 4:51 PM on November 15 and 4:43 PM on November 30 [3][4].

If you're walking a beach or trail, plan to be back at the car by then. <!-- nocite -->

## Beaches and the National Seashore in November

Cape Cod National Seashore is open year-round, though from November through April some visitor facilities are closed [10]. When we checked on November 10, 2026, the Park Service's current-conditions page showed [8]:

- **Beaches:** Seashore beaches such as Head of the Meadow and Herring Cove are listed as open, but restrooms and showers are closed (the vault toilets in those two beaches' parking lots are open year-round), and beach entrance fee collection and annual pass sales have ended for the season [8]. Seashore entrance fees are charged only seasonally, at park beaches [9].
- **Lifeguards:** they're typically on duty at the six Seashore beaches only from late June through Labor Day [10].
- **Visitor centers:** Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham, the Seashore's year-round visitor center [11], is listed as open from 9 AM to 4:30 PM [8]. The Province Lands Visitor Center in Provincetown is open May through October [11] and has closed for the season, though its outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting [8][11].
- **Trails:** all eleven walking trails in the park are listed as open [8]. Leashed pets are allowed on the Nauset and Province Lands bike trails from November 1 through April 30, and on the Head of the Meadow Bike Trail year-round [8].

**Hunting season.** Hunting for deer, upland game and migratory waterfowl is permitted in the Seashore, generally in accordance with Massachusetts state hunting regulations, and all hunting is prohibited from March 1 through August 31, except for the spring turkey hunt [12]. Hunting and loaded weapons are prohibited on or within 500 feet of established bike and official nature trails [12].

If you're walking off-trail in the woods this time of year, wearing bright colors is common sense. <!-- nocite -->

**Town beaches.** Off-season rules are set town by town. In Eastham, for example, stickers or day passes were required at its bay beaches, freshwater ponds and pull-off areas only from June 20, 2026, through Labor Day, September 7 [13].

Check each town's page before you park. For current conditions, see [Cape Cod beach conditions today](/cape-cod-beach-conditions-today.html). <!-- nocite -->

## Ferries, whale watches and buses: what's still running

**Island ferries.** The Steamship Authority runs daily, year-round service from Cape Cod to Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket [14]; its current Martha's Vineyard schedule runs from October 23, 2026, to January 5, 2027 [14]. Hy-Line Cruises has run year-round Hyannis–Nantucket service since December 1995 [16], but its Hyannis–Martha's Vineyard ferry has ended for the season [15].

**Boston and Plymouth to Provincetown.** Bay State Cruise Company's fast ferry runs weekends only through November 15, 2026, and it says trips are 25 to 35 minutes longer than usual because of a right whale Slow Zone that stays in place through that last sailing [17]. City Cruises' Provincetown Fast Ferry runs seasonally, mid-May through mid-October [18], and Captain John Boats' Plymouth–Provincetown ferry is closed for the season [19].

**Whale watching.** Hyannis Whale Watcher Cruises' last trip of 2026 was Sunday, November 1 [20], and Captain John Boats is closed until spring [19]. Right whales can show up early, though: last season's first right whale was reported from Race Point Beach by a member of the public on October 26, 2025, and the first aerial survey of the season flew on November 3, 2025 [45].

Our [winter whales and seals guide](/guides/whales-and-seals-cape-cod-winter/) covers where to look from shore and how close you can legally get. <!-- nocite -->

**Cold-stunned sea turtles.** Mass Audubon says sea turtle strandings on Cape Cod mainly happen from November to mid-January, mostly along Cape Cod Bay [46]. If you find one, call its Wellfleet Bay hotline at 508-349-2615, option 2 [46].

**Buses.** CCRTA's FLEX (Harwich–Provincetown), H2O (Hyannis–Orleans) and Sealine (Hyannis–Falmouth–Woods Hole) routes run Monday through Saturday on the schedule for September 8, 2026, to January 22, 2027 [21][22][23]. All three run a Saturday schedule on Veterans Day, November 11, and have no service on Thanksgiving [21][22][23]. The Provincetown–North Truro Shuttle ran through October 11, 2026 [24], and the Hyannis Trolley is a summer service, Memorial Day to Labor Day [25]. CCRTA's home page advertises free fares on all fixed routes [26].

**Driving over?** Check [live Sagamore and Bourne bridge traffic](/cape-cod-bridge-traffic.html) before you leave, especially on Thanksgiving weekend. <!-- nocite -->

## Museums, cranberries and markets

- **Cape Cod Museum of Natural History, Brewster:** fall 2026 hours are Wednesday through Sunday, 10 AM to 3 PM, with last admission at 2:30 PM; it's closed on Veterans Day, November 11, and Thanksgiving Day, November 26 [28].
- **Heritage Museums & Gardens, Sandwich:** now closed for its main season [29]. It reopens for Gardens Aglow, 4:30 to 8:30 PM, starting Friday, November 27 [50]; all dates are in our [holiday events guide](/guides/cape-cod-holiday-events-2026/).
- **Cranberries:** the Cape Cod Cranberry Growers' Association says the harvest runs once a year from mid-September through early November [27].
- **Orleans Farmers' Market:** outdoors every Saturday from 9 AM to noon at 19 Old Colony Way through November 21 [30]. It's year-round, but it takes a break the week after Thanksgiving; from December through April it moves inside to 5 Namskaket Road, 10 AM to noon [30]. It has also moved inside to 5 Namskaket Road in wet weather this fall, so check its site before you go [30].
- **Falmouth Farmers Market:** Sundays from 10 AM to 1 PM in the Mullen Hall School parking lot, through November 15, 2026 [31].

## Thanksgiving week: November 25–29

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 [28].

- **Chatham Turkey Trot:** a 5K run/walk on Thanksgiving morning to benefit the Lower Cape Outreach Council [42], starting at 8 AM [42] from the Oyster Pond parking lot, 80 Stage Harbor Road [41].
