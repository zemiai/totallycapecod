## December weather on Cape Cod

NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals for Hyannis (Barnstable Municipal Airport) compare late fall and early winter [1]:

| Month | Average high | Average low | Precipitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| November | 53.5°F [1] | 37.3°F [1] | 4.13 in [1] |
| December | 45.2°F [1] | 29.3°F [1] | 4.68 in [1] |
| January | 39.9°F [1] | 24.1°F [1] | 3.90 in [1] |

December's normal mean temperature at Hyannis is 37.2°F, down from 45.4°F in November [1].

**Storms.** The National Weather Service says nor'easters, named for their northeast winds along the coast, can happen any time of year but are most frequent and most violent between September and April [7].

**Ocean temperatures.** See today's readings on our [Cape Cod water temperature page](/cape-cod-water-temperature-today.html). <!-- nocite -->

## Beaches and the National Seashore in December

Cape Cod National Seashore is open year-round, though from November through April some visitor facilities are closed [9]. When we checked on November 17, 2026, the Park Service's current-conditions page showed [8]:

- **Beaches:** Coast Guard, Nauset Light, Marconi, Head of the Meadow, Race Point and Herring Cove beaches are listed as open, with restrooms and showers closed and beach entrance fee collection and annual pass sales ended for the season [8]. The vault toilets in the Head of the Meadow and Herring Cove parking lots are open year-round [8].
- **Lifeguards:** they're typically on duty at the six Seashore beaches only from late June through Labor Day [9].
- **Visitor centers:** Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham, the Seashore's year-round visitor center [10], is listed as open from 9 AM to 4:30 PM [8]. Its satellite restroom building in the parking lot is closing for work beginning December 1, with portable toilets available [8]. The Province Lands Visitor Center in Provincetown has closed for the season, though restrooms in its parking area are open year-round [8], and its outdoor decks are open all year, weather permitting [10].
- **Trails:** all eleven walking trails are listed as open [8]. Beginning November 30, a small stretch of the Nauset Marsh Trail around the bridge will be closed for about a month while park staff replace the wooden planks [8]. Leashed pets are allowed on the Nauset and Province Lands bike trails from November 1 through April 30 [8].
- **Oversand driving:** the ORV corridor is in its limited-access offseason and reopens in April 2027 [8].

**Hunting season.** Hunting is permitted in the Seashore according to state hunting regulations, except as further restricted by federal rules [11]; all hunting is prohibited from March 1 through August 31, except for the spring turkey hunt [11]. Hunting or carrying a loaded weapon is prohibited on or within 500 feet of established bike and official nature trails [11].

Off-trail in the woods, bright colors are a sensible choice this time of year. <!-- nocite -->

## Ferries, buses and wildlife

**Island ferries.** The Steamship Authority runs daily, year-round service from Cape Cod to Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket [12]; its current Martha's Vineyard schedule runs from October 23, 2026, to January 5, 2027 [12]. Hy-Line Cruises has run year-round Hyannis–Nantucket service since December 1995 [13], but its Hyannis–Martha's Vineyard ferry has ended for the season [14].

**Buses.** CCRTA's FLEX (Harwich–Provincetown), H2O (Hyannis–Orleans) and Sealine (Hyannis–Falmouth–Woods Hole) routes run Monday through Saturday on the schedule for September 8, 2026, to January 22, 2027 [15][16][17]. None of the three runs on Christmas or New Year's Day [15][16][17]. CCRTA advertises free fares on all fixed routes [15].

**Right whales.** NOAA Fisheries listed a voluntary right whale Slow Zone in Cape Cod Bay effective November 14–29, 2026 [18]. Slow Zones are based on visual and acoustic detections of right whales, and mariners are asked to avoid the area or slow to 10 knots or less [18]. Each Slow Zone lasts 15 days after a whale is detected [18], so check NOAA's current list. The federal Cape Cod Bay Seasonal Management Area, where most vessels 65 feet or longer must go 10 knots or less, doesn't begin until January 1 and runs to May 15 [18].

Our [winter whales and seals guide](/guides/whales-and-seals-cape-cod-winter/) covers where to look from shore and how close you can legally get. <!-- nocite -->

**Cold-stunned sea turtles.** Mass Audubon says sea turtle strandings on Cape Cod mainly happen from November to mid-January, mostly along Cape Cod Bay [19]. If you find one, don't put it back in the water; move it above the high tide line, cover it with seaweed, mark the spot and call the Wellfleet Bay hotline at 508-349-2615, option 2 [19].

**Driving over?** Check [live Sagamore and Bourne bridge traffic](/cape-cod-bridge-traffic.html), and see our guide to [the best times to cross the bridges](/guides/sagamore-bourne-bridge-traffic-best-times/). <!-- nocite -->

## Museums and markets open in December

- **Cape Cod Museum of Natural History, Brewster:** fall 2026 hours are Wednesday through Sunday, 10 AM to 3 PM, with last admission at 2:30 PM, and it's closed for the winter holidays from Monday, December 21, through Saturday, December 26 [21], and on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day [21].
- **Sandwich Glass Museum:** open seven days a week, 9:30 AM to 5 PM, with live glassblowing demonstrations hourly through December 30 [23]. It's closed Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, then closed December 31 through January 31 [23]. Adult admission is $15 [23].
- **JFK Hyannis Museum:** open December 3–6 and 10–12 from 10 AM to 6 PM, December 17–19 and 21–23 from 10 AM to 4 PM, and December 28–30 from 10 AM to 4 PM; closed December 24, 25, 26 and 31 [24].
- **Cahoon Museum of American Art, Cotuit:** open Wednesday through Sunday, 10 AM to 4 PM, until its season ends on December 20, 2026 [25]. Adult admission is $12 [25].
- **Cape Cod Museum of Art, Dennis:** open year-round, Tuesday through Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM and Sunday noon to 4 PM [26]. Its *Wrap It Up!* show of small works runs from November 12, 2026, through January 3, 2027, free of admission [26].
- **Cultural Center of Cape Cod, South Yarmouth:** *Small Wonders 2026*, a juried show of art no more than 12 inches on any side, fills its Great Hall through Saturday, December 19 [27].
- **Provincetown Art Association and Museum:** open Wednesday through Sunday, noon to 5 PM; general admission is $15, free for members and children 17 and under [28].
- **Heritage Museums & Gardens, Sandwich:** Gardens Aglow runs 4:30 to 8:30 PM on set nights through December 27, with no dates on December 24 or 25 [22]. Full details are in our [holiday events guide](/guides/cape-cod-holiday-events-2026/). <!-- nocite -->
- **Orleans Farmers' Market:** from December through April it's indoors at 5 Namskaket Road, Orleans, from 10 AM to noon [20]. The market runs 50 weeks a year, taking a break the week after Thanksgiving and Christmas [20].

## Christmas week: what changes

- **Buses:** no CCRTA FLEX, H2O or Sealine service on Christmas Day, Friday, December 25 [15][16][17]. Trips marked with an asterisk on those schedules don't run on Saturdays or during school vacations [15][16][17].
- **Museums:** the Cape Cod Museum of Natural History is closed December 21–26 and on New Year's Eve [21], the Sandwich Glass Museum is closed Christmas Eve and Christmas Day [23], and the JFK Hyannis Museum is closed December 24–26 [24].
- **Gardens Aglow:** it isn't open December 24 or 25, but it is open Saturday and Sunday, December 26–27 [22].
- **Still on after Christmas:** A Taste of Jazz on Saturday, December 26 [39], and *Frozen* through January 3, 2027 [30].

Call restaurants, shops and inns before you go; holiday-week hours vary. <!-- nocite -->
